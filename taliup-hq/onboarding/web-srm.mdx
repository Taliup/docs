Taliup POS acts as a sales recording system (SRS / MEV) for Revenu Québec's WEB-SRM. Taliup HQ stores the merchant's Revenu Québec identifiers. The POS uses those identifiers on every fiscal request, including the certificate that enrols each device.

Complete this page before you enrol a device. If HQ is not configured, Taliup POS shows **This business has no WEB-SRM configuration from Taliup HQ.**

## How to get here

Entity sidebar → **Web-SRM**.

The menu item is visible only to an **Admin** when the Entity plan includes the **Web-SRM** feature. If you do not see it, add the feature on [Plans & Features](/taliup-hq/onboarding/plans-features).

## Enable WEB-SRM

<Warning>
  The first time you enable WEB-SRM, Taliup HQ creates default Québec **GST** (5%) and **QST** (9.975%) taxes if they do not already exist. Any other catalog taxes are removed and replaced with those Québec taxes. Confirm the catalog is ready for that change before you continue.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Web-SRM">
    From the Entity sidebar, click **Web-SRM**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Web-SRM">
    Turn on **Enable Web-SRM**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Revenu Québec credentials">
    Fill in the mandatory fields. Use the values Revenu Québec issued to the merchant.

    | Field | Example | Notes |
    |---|---|---|
    | **User Identification Number** | `5678912340` | Merchant user identification number from Revenu Québec. |
    | **User Authorization Code** | `X1X1-X1X1` | Format `XXXX-XXXX`. |
    | **QST Number** | `5678912340TQ0001` | Québec sales tax number. |
    | **GST Number** | `123456789RT0001` | Federal GST/HST number. |
    | **Billing File Number** | `ER0001` | Revenu Québec billing file number. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and save">
    Click **Save Changes**. On first enable, confirm the **Confirm Web-SRM Activation** modal, then click **Confirm and Enable Web-SRM**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can still adjust GST and QST later in catalog tax settings. Other tax types are not used for WEB-SRM billing.
</Note>

## Next step — enrol each device

HQ configuration alone does not enrol a device.

- **New device** — complete [device setup](/taliup-pos/index), then [enrol the device](/taliup-pos/web-srm).
- **Merchant already live on Taliup POS** — follow [Start using WEB-SRM on an existing merchant](/taliup-pos/web-srm/switch-live-merchant). They must log out and log back in after you save WEB-SRM here.
- **Merchant still on standalone Taliup MEV** — follow [Migrate from MEV](#migrate-from-mev) below, then enrol each device.

Repeat enrolment on each device. Each device receives its own Revenu Québec certificate and device identifier.

## Migrate from MEV

Taliup POS now includes WEB-SRM (MEV) support, so a merchant no longer needs the standalone **Taliup MEV** app on their terminal. Remove Taliup MEV and install Taliup POS in its place.

<Note>
  Taliup MEV runs only on the **PAX A920 Pro**. Repeat this procedure once for every terminal under the merchant that still has Taliup MEV installed.
</Note>

### Before you start

- Complete **Enable WEB-SRM** above. Taliup POS shows **This business has no WEB-SRM configuration from Taliup HQ** on login until this is done.
- Access to **PaxStore** with the correct **Administrator Center** and **Reseller** account.
- The terminal powered on and connected to the internet.
- Confirm the Reseller or Merchant is **subscribed** to **Taliup POS** in the PaxStore marketplace before you push it.

### Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to PaxStore">
    Log in to PaxStore with your administrator credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the merchant">
    In **Terminal Management**, search for the merchant or its Reseller. Open **Devices**, then the **Terminal List** tab.

    <Frame caption="Search for the reseller or merchant in Terminal Management.">
      ![PaxStore Terminal Management search for Taliup with Taliup M selected](/images/taliup-hq/web-srm/paxstore-search-reseller.png)
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="Reseller Detail with the Devices card and the Terminal List tab. Click a terminal row to open its details.">
      ![PaxStore Reseller Detail showing the Devices card, Accessories card, and a Terminal List of PAX A920 Pro devices](/images/taliup-hq/web-srm/paxstore-devices-terminal-list.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the affected terminal">
    From the Terminal List, click the terminal that still has Taliup MEV installed to open its **Terminal Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to App & Firmware">
    Click the **App & Firmware** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Uninstall Taliup MEV">
    Under **Installed Apps**, find **Taliup MEV** and click the trash can icon under **Type & Action** to uninstall it.

    <Frame caption="App & Firmware on Terminal Details. Taliup MEV appears under Installed Apps with a trash can in Type & Action.">
      ![PaxStore App & Firmware tab listing Taliup MEV 1.1.6 under Installed Apps with an uninstall action](/images/taliup-hq/web-srm/paxstore-taliup-mev.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install Taliup POS">
    Push **Taliup POS** to the terminal. Follow the **A920 Pro** app push order in [PaxStore setup](/taliup-hq/devices/pax/paxstore#3-pax-a920-pro-push-apps) — **BroadPOS Manager**, then the acquirer BroadPOS app, then **Taliup POS**, confirming each **Push Task** shows **Success** before moving on.

    <Tip>
      If BroadPOS Manager and the acquirer BroadPOS app are already installed and working from the Taliup MEV setup, you only need to push **Taliup POS**.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat for each affected terminal">
    Repeat steps 3–6 for every other terminal under this merchant that still has Taliup MEV installed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Check

| Check | Expected |
|---|---|
| Installed Apps | **Taliup MEV** is gone; **Taliup POS** shows a **Success** Push Task |
| First login on the terminal | Employee login screen appears with no WEB-SRM configuration error |
| WEB-SRM enrolment | **Enrol this device** appears and completes |

### Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| No trash can icon next to Taliup MEV | The app may be a **System App**, which can't be removed from Installed Apps. Confirm you're looking at the right row under **Type & Action** — only **App Store** and **Third Party** entries are removable. |
| Taliup POS push never completes | Confirm the terminal is online in PaxStore, check the **Push Task** status, verify **Taliup POS** is subscribed in the marketplace, then reboot the terminal and retry. |
| No card prompt after switching | BroadPOS is missing or misconfigured. Re-push **BroadPOS Manager**, then the acquirer BroadPOS app with VAR parameters, then **Taliup POS** — in that order. |
| **This business has no WEB-SRM configuration from Taliup HQ** on first login | Complete **Enable WEB-SRM** above, then log out and log back in on the terminal. |

With Taliup POS installed, staff can log in on the terminal for the first time and enrol it with WEB-SRM.
