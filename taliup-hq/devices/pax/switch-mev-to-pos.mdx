Taliup POS now includes WEB-SRM (MEV) support, so a merchant no longer needs the standalone **Taliup MEV** app on their terminal. Use this page to remove Taliup MEV and install Taliup POS in its place.

<Note>
  Taliup MEV runs only on the **PAX A920 Pro**. Repeat this procedure once for every terminal under the merchant that still has Taliup MEV installed.
</Note>

## Before you start

- Access to **PaxStore** with the correct **Administrator Center** and **Reseller** account.
- The terminal powered on and connected to the internet.
- Confirm the Reseller or Merchant is **subscribed** to **Taliup POS** in the PaxStore marketplace before you push it.
- If the merchant isn't already configured for WEB-SRM in Taliup HQ, finish [WEB-SRM settings](/taliup-hq/onboarding/web-srm) first. Taliup POS shows **This business has no WEB-SRM configuration from Taliup HQ** on login until this is done.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to PaxStore">
    Log in to PaxStore with your administrator credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the merchant">
    In **Terminal Management**, search for the merchant (or its Reseller) to open its Terminal List.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the affected terminal">
    From the Terminal List, click the terminal that still has Taliup MEV installed to open its **Terminal Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to App & Firmware">
    Click the **App & Firmware** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Uninstall Taliup MEV">
    Under **Installed Apps**, find **Taliup MEV** and click the trash can icon under **Type & Action** to uninstall it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install Taliup POS">
    Push **Taliup POS** to the terminal. Follow the **A920 Pro** app push order in [PaxStore setup](/taliup-hq/devices/pax/paxstore#3-pax-a920-pro-push-apps) — **BroadPOS Manager**, then the acquirer BroadPOS app, then **Taliup POS**, confirming each **Push Task** shows **Success** before moving on.

    <Tip>
      If BroadPOS Manager and the acquirer BroadPOS app are already installed and working from the Taliup MEV setup, you only need to push **Taliup POS**.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat for each affected terminal">
    Repeat steps 3–6 for every other terminal under this merchant that still has Taliup MEV installed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check

| Check | Expected |
|---|---|
| Installed Apps | **Taliup MEV** is gone; **Taliup POS** shows a **Success** Push Task |
| First login on the terminal | Employee login screen appears with no WEB-SRM configuration error |
| WEB-SRM enrolment | **Enrol this device** appears and completes |

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| No trash can icon next to Taliup MEV | The app may be a **System App**, which can't be removed from Installed Apps. Confirm you're looking at the right row under **Type & Action** — only **App Store** and **Third Party** entries are removable. |
| Taliup POS push never completes | Confirm the terminal is online in PaxStore, check the **Push Task** status, verify **Taliup POS** is subscribed in the marketplace, then reboot the terminal and retry. |
| No card prompt after switching | BroadPOS is missing or misconfigured. Re-push **BroadPOS Manager**, then the acquirer BroadPOS app with VAR parameters, then **Taliup POS** — in that order. |
| **This business has no WEB-SRM configuration from Taliup HQ** on first login | Finish [WEB-SRM settings](/taliup-hq/onboarding/web-srm) in Taliup HQ, then log out and log back in on the terminal. |
