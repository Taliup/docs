When no time works for a client, they can join a waitlist from your booking page. When a matching time frees up, Taliup texts and emails the first clients in line a link to book it. The first to book gets the time.

The **Waitlist** page in Taliup HQ shows who is waiting, who was alerted, and whose dates have passed. From it, you can send a client the next opening or book them yourself.

## How to get here

Sidebar → **Appointments** → **Waitlist**. You need the **Waitlist** feature, which comes with **Calendar**.

| Permission | What it allows |
|---|---|
| **View Appointments** | Open the waitlist and each client's details and history |
| **Manage Appointments** | **Send next opening**, **Book for client**, **Put back in line**, and **Remove** |
| **Manage Settings** | Turn the waitlist on from the page, or in Calendar settings |

The calendar's **More actions** menu also links to **Waitlist**.

## Turn on the waitlist

Clients join the waitlist from your [online booking](/taliup-hq/features/online-booking) page, so online booking must be on.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Calendar settings">
    Go to [Calendar settings](/taliup-hq/features/calendar-settings) and open the **Online booking** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Turn on **Let clients join a waitlist**. It shows once online booking is on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the waitlist is off, the **Waitlist** page says so. Click **Turn on** to switch it on right there, or **Waitlist settings** to open the setting.

## How clients join

On the booking page, the date and time step shows **No time that works? Join the waitlist**. The staff step links to the waitlist too.

The join page starts on the service, day, staff member, and location the client was looking at. The client chooses:

| Question | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Which days?** | A date range |
| **What time of day?** | **Any time**, **Morning** (before 12), **Afternoon** (12 to 5), or **Evening** (after 5) |
| **Who with?** | A staff member, or any staff |
| **Where should we send the alert?** | First name, last name, email, and mobile phone |
| **Anything we should know?** | An optional note for your team |

The client clicks **Join the waitlist** and sees **You're on the waitlist**. Joining is free. Nothing is booked or charged until the client books from an alert.

Joining again for the same service updates the client's request. Joining never replaces the phone number already on an existing client's profile.

## When a time frees up

A time frees up when an appointment is cancelled, deleted, or moved to another time, service, staff member, or location. This applies wherever the change was made: Taliup HQ, Taliup POS, or the client's own cancellation.

Taliup then looks for waiting clients whose service, dates, time of day, staff member, and location fit the freed time. The first three in line are texted and emailed a link to book it.

- The link opens the booking page on that time, at the freed time's location.
- The booking details step fills in the client's name, email, and phone from their waitlist request. They can still change them.
- When the client books, they come off the waitlist. If the booking needs a deposit or a card, they come off only once the payment goes through.
- A client who doesn't book within 24 hours goes back in line for the next opening.
- Every alert has a **Leave the waitlist** link.

## The Waitlist page

Clients are sorted into three tabs, each with a count.

| Tab | Who is in it |
|---|---|
| **Waiting** | Clients waiting for a time. A client who was alerted before and went back in line shows **Back in line · alerted** and the date |
| **Alerted** | Clients who were sent a time and haven't booked yet. The **Offered time** column shows the time sent, and when they go back in line |
| **Dates passed** | Clients whose last wanted date is over |

Search by client or service, and filter by **Service**, **Staff**, and **Location**. Click **Reset** to clear the filters. Click **How it works** for a short explanation in a side panel. The settings icon opens **Waitlist settings**.

### Actions

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Send next opening** | Finds the earliest open time that fits the client's dates, time of day, staff member, and location, and texts and emails it to them. If nothing fits yet, you see a message saying so |
| **Book for client** | Opens the calendar's booking form with the client, service, staff member, and location filled in, on the first wanted date that hasn't passed |
| **Put back in line** | Shown for alerted clients. Puts them back in line for the next opening, for example when someone else took the time |
| **Remove** | Takes the client off the waitlist after you confirm. They stop getting alerts but can join again from your booking page |

**Send next opening** isn't shown once a client's dates have passed.

## Client details

Click a row to open the client's details in a drawer:

- The service, staff member, and status
- For alerted clients, the time offered, when it was sent, and when they go back in line if they don't book
- **Wants**: the dates, time of day, location, and the client's note
- **Contact**: phone and email, with a **Profile** link to the client's record
- **History**: when they joined or updated their request, each alert (by text, email, or both), going back in line, booking, and removal. Actions taken by staff show who did them

The drawer's footer has **Remove**, **Book for client**, and **Send next opening**.
