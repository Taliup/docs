Turn gift cards on for each location, keep digital card numbers ready, and review the cards you have sold. A merchant has one gift card program shared across all its locations, so a card sold at one store works at the others.

Staff sell, reload, and accept gift cards on the terminal. See [Gift cards on Taliup POS](/taliup-pos/register/gift-cards).

## How to get here

Sidebar → **Gift Cards**. You need the **Gift Cards** feature and the **View Transactions** permission.

## Turn gift cards on for a location

Under **Locations**, turn gift cards on for each store. A location needs at least one terminal. Activation goes through secure checkout, like Loyalty.

Use **Manage Subscriptions** to review or cancel the subscription.

## Digital numbers

Digital gift cards use numbers from a shared pool. When a register sells a digital card, it takes the next number and emails the card to the customer after the sale is paid. The customer can add it to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

**Digital Gift Cards Available** shows how many numbers are ready to sell.

**Add numbers now** adds a one-time batch. Pick a **Quantity**, then **Add numbers**. Your card on file is charged per number before they are added. These are digital numbers only. Physical cards are not ordered.

**Automatic refill of digital numbers** tops up the pool when it runs low:

1. Turn on **Refill digital numbers automatically**.
2. Set **Minimum digital numbers left** and **Digital numbers to add**. The minimum must be lower than the number added.
3. Click **Save automatic refill** and confirm. Each refill charges your card on file.

When automatic refill is **Off**, numbers are added only when you add them yourself.

## Cards

**Cards** lists sold cards once at least one location is on. The summary shows **Total Cards**, **Active Cards**, **Total Balance**, and **Last Activity**.

Search by the last 4 digits, name, email, or phone. Filter by status or location, then click **Apply**.

Click **View** to open **Gift Card Details**:

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Card Details** | Balance, status, and expiry. Use **Refresh Balance** to check the live balance |
| **Account History** | Every load, redemption, reload, and refund, by location, with totals |
| **Delivery** | For emailed cards: recipient, status, and when it was sent. Use **Resend** to send it again |

**Deactivate** stops a card from being used.

## Reports

Sales reports show **Gift Card Purchases** and **Gift Card Refunds**.
