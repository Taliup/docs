Timesheets show the hours staff clock on Taliup POS. Use them to check hours, fix a missed clock-out, or add a shift someone forgot to clock. Pay runs use the same hours.

## How to get here

Sidebar → **Team** → **Timesheets**. You need the **Timesheets** feature and the **Manage Employees** permission.

From an employee, **Timesheets** in the row menu or on their profile opens this page filtered to them.

## Pick the dates and people

The page opens on **This week**.

- Use the arrows (**Earlier**, **Later**) to move by the same length of time.
- Open the dates to pick **This week**, **Last week**, **This month**, **Last month**, or **Custom dates** (**From**, **To**, then **Show these dates**).
- Pick an **Employee**, or **Everyone**. **Reset** clears the employee.

The dates and employee stay in the page address, so you can bookmark or share the view.

## Summary

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Hours worked** | Finished shifts only |
| **Labour cost** | Hours × each person's hourly rate. Flags shifts with no hourly rate |
| **Shifts** | How many shifts are in the dates |
| **Clocked in now** | Who is still clocked in, or **Nobody** |

Hours are exact to the minute, the same way pay runs count them.

## Shifts tab

**Shifts** lists every shift with the **Employee**, **Date**, **Clocked in – out** times, **Hours**, **Labour cost** and **Status**. A shift that ends after midnight shows **+1 day**. A shift still running shows **Clocked in**.

Click a shift to open it.

## Totals by employee tab

**Totals by employee** shows each person's **Shifts**, **Hours**, **Hourly rate** and **Labour cost** for the dates, with a **Totals** row. Hours and cost count finished shifts. Shifts still running count once they end.

Click **View shifts** to see one person's shifts.

## Add a shift

Use this for a shift someone forgot to clock.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the drawer">
    Click **Add shift**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the shift">
    Choose the **Employee**. Set the **Date** and **Time** for **Clocked in** and **Clocked out**. For a shift past midnight, pick the next day for the clock-out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The drawer previews the hours and labour cost. Click **Save shift**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The clock-out has to be after the clock-in.

## Fix a shift

Click the shift, change the times, then click **Save shift**.

To remove it, click **Delete shift** and confirm. The shift is removed from timesheets and from pay runs that aren't finalized.

## Clock someone out

If someone forgot to clock out, their shift is still running.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the shift">
    Click their shift. The drawer shows when they clocked in and how long so far.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the clock-out time">
    Choose **Now**, or **At another time** (for example, when they left) and set the date and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clock out">
    Click **Clock out**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also use **Clock out now** on the [employees list](/taliup-hq/features/employees), or **Clock out** on the employee's profile.

## Export

Click **Export CSV** to download the shifts for the dates and employee you picked. The file lists each shift with the employee, date, clock-in, clock-out, hours, hourly rate and labour cost.
