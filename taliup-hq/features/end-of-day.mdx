**End of Day** in Taliup HQ is the same merchant daily document you can print from Taliup POS. Use it to review the day in the browser, then generate a PDF.

<Warning>
This document does **not** close card batches or settle terminals. It is documentation only.
</Warning>

You can also generate and print this report on a terminal. See [End of Day in Taliup POS](/taliup-pos/transactions/end-of-day).

## Before you start

- Your plan includes the **End of Day Report** feature.
- You need **View Reports** to open the page. Generating a document needs **Manage Reports**. Deleting generated documents needs **Delete Reports**.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, open **End of day**, under **Insights**.

## Choose the day and filters

On the **Report** tab, use **Business day** or the arrows to change the date. If your business has several locations, select **Location** to narrow the report to one of them. Both changes refresh the report immediately. Changing location includes all its devices.

To narrow the report further, open **Devices** or **Employees**, select one or more entries, and select **Apply** inside that filter. Leave **All devices** and **All employees** selected for a store-wide report. Use **Reset** to return to today with all locations, devices and employees.

## Save or print the report

1. Review the date, filters and totals. Select **Print** to print the report currently on screen.
2. To keep a copy with these filters, select **Save as PDF**. This requires **Manage Reports**.
3. After **PDF saved** appears, select **Open PDF**. You can download or print it from your PDF viewer.

## Find a saved PDF

Open **Saved PDFs** to find reports saved from HQ or a register. Search or filter by date, then select **Open PDF** on the matching row. With **Delete Reports**, you can also delete one PDF or select several, choose **Delete selected** and confirm with **Delete**.
