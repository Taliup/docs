**End of Day** in Taliup HQ is the same merchant daily document you can print from Taliup POS. Use it to review the day in the browser, then generate a PDF.

<Warning>
This document does **not** close card batches or settle terminals. It is documentation only.
</Warning>

You can also generate and print this report on a terminal. See [End of Day in Taliup POS](/taliup-pos/transactions/end-of-day).

## How to get here

In the merchant sidebar, open **End of Day**.

You need the **End of Day Report** feature on the plan and the **View Reports** permission.

## Filters

Filters default to every terminal and every employee. Narrow them when you only need part of the store.

| Filter | What it includes |
|---|---|
| **Business Date** | The day to document |
| **Location** | One location, or **All locations** |
| **Terminal** | One or more activated devices. **All terminals** selects every device in the list. |
| **Employee** | One or more staff users. **All employees** selects every user in the list. |

Select at least one terminal and one employee, then tap **Apply Filters** to refresh the on-screen summary.

<Tip>
Leave **All terminals** and **All employees** selected for a store-wide document.
</Tip>

## Generate a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the date and scope">
    Choose the **Business Date**, then confirm location, terminals, and employees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters">
    Tap **Apply Filters** to load the summary for that scope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Tap **Generate Document**. Taliup HQ saves a PDF you can view, download, or print.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The document lists the selected terminals and employees by name when you did not choose all of them.

## Generated documents

The generated list shows earlier documents for this entity. Use **View**, **Download PDF**, or **Print Report** on a row.

New documents keep the filters you used when you generated them. Older documents without a saved snapshot recalculate when you open them.
