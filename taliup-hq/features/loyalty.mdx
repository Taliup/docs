Reward repeat customers with points they redeem at the register. Taliup HQ holds the program: which locations take part, how points are earned and redeemed, the card stock, and the members.

Staff enrol clients, earn, and redeem on the terminal. See [Loyalty on Taliup POS](/taliup-pos/register/loyalty).

## How to get here

Sidebar → **Loyalty**. You need the **Loyalty** feature and the **View Transactions** permission.

## Activate a location

Loyalty is billed per location, per month, and activated through secure checkout.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the location">
    Under **Locations**, find the store. A location needs at least one terminal. Otherwise you see **Add a terminal first**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    Click **Activate**. Secure checkout opens in a new window. Allow pop-ups if the browser blocks it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Complete payment. The status moves from **Checkout pending** to **Setting up**, then **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you close checkout early, click **Resume checkout**. Use **Manage Subscriptions** to review or cancel the subscription.

## Program rules

Under **Program Rules**, set how members earn and redeem. Taliup computes points on the order subtotal after discounts, before tax. Rewards apply as a pre-tax discount.

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Program name** | Shown on the member card page and wallet pass |
| **Points per 1.00 spent** | `1` = one point per dollar. `0.5` = one point per two dollars |
| **Minimum purchase to earn** | Optional. Leave blank if every purchase earns |
| **Rounding** | **Round down**, **Round to nearest**, or **Round up** |
| **Points per reward** | How many points make one reward |
| **Reward value** | What one reward is worth |
| **Minimum points to redeem** | Optional. Leave blank if any reward can be redeemed |
| **Bonus points for new members** | Optional welcome bonus, added when a new member's card is issued |

The preview under the form shows an example, such as *A $50.00 purchase earns 50 points. Every 100 points are worth $5.00.* Click **Save rules**.

The welcome bonus applies to members who join after you set it.

## Cards

Members carry either a printed card or a digital card number.

| Card | How it is used |
|---|---|
| **Printed** | You hand the card over and staff scan it at enrolment. It is assigned only when scanned |
| **Digital** | Assigned to members who do not take a printed card. The card page and wallet pass are emailed |

The summary shows **Printed Cards in Stock** and how many digital numbers are ready.

**Add card numbers** adds a batch of printed card numbers. Pick a **Quantity**, then **Add numbers**. Your card on file is charged per number before they are added.

**Automatic refill of digital numbers** tops up digital numbers when they run low:

1. Turn on **Refill digital numbers automatically**.
2. Set **Minimum digital numbers left** and **Digital numbers to add**. The minimum must be lower than the number added.
3. Click **Save automatic refill** and confirm. Each refill charges your card on file. Physical cards are not ordered.

## Members

**Members** lists everyone enrolled, with points and lifetime earned. Search by name, email, phone, or card.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Adjust** | Add or remove points with a reason. Use a negative number to remove. The balance cannot go below zero |
| **Send card** | Emails the card page and wallet pass to the member |
| **Replace card** | Moves the points to a new card. Scan or key the new card, or leave it empty to assign the next card from stock (lost card). The old number stops working |

**Recent Activity** lists the latest earns, redemptions, voids, enrolments, adjustments, and welcome bonuses.

A client's record in **Clients** also has a **Loyalty** tab with their points history.

## Member card page and wallet passes

Each member gets a card page with their points and a QR code to show at the counter. From the page, they can add the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

The card page and passes use the merchant's brand colour and logo.

## Rewards signup page

Customers can join on their own from a public **Join rewards** page. They enter their name, phone, and email, and their card arrives by email. Members can use **Email me my card** to get it again.

Terminals can print a signup code that links to this page on the sale receipt. See **Print rewards signup** in [General settings](/taliup-pos/settings/general).

## Loyalty report

In **Reports**, generate a **Loyalty** report for a date range, location, terminal, or employee. It shows:

- **Points Earned**, **Points Redeemed**, **Reward Value**, **Eligible Sales**, and **Net Points Change**
- **Membership**: active and new members, points outstanding, reward liability, voids, adjustments, and declines
- Breakdowns **By Location**, **By Employee**, and **By Day**, plus **Top Members**
