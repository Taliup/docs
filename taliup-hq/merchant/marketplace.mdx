The **Add-ons** page shows your plan and lets you add features to it, one at a time or as bundles. Add-ons are billed together each month on your add-on subscription.

## Before you start

- **Add-ons** is available only when your plan allows feature add-ons. Your provider decides this. If you do not see **Add-ons**, contact your provider to change your plan.
- Only people with the **Admin** role in Taliup HQ see **Add-ons** in the sidebar and can buy add-ons.
- **Billing and statements** and **Go to billing** need the **Manage Subscriptions** permission.

## How to get here

At the bottom of the sidebar, open **Add-ons**.

When you open a page for a feature your plan does not include, Taliup HQ shows **Not on your plan yet**. Click **See all add-ons** to open this page. If you are not an Admin, the card asks you to contact an Admin of your business.

## What the page shows

- **Your plan**: your plan's name and the features it includes.
- **Add-on subscription**: once you have add-ons, the monthly total, the card and next billing date, and each add-on with its price. An add-on being cancelled shows the date it ends.
- **Bundles**: several features together for less. Features you already have show **You have this**. Add-ons you already pay for that are in a bundle come off your bill.
- **Single features**: each feature with its price and status.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Active** | The feature is on your add-on subscription. |
| **Included** | The feature is part of your plan. |
| **Add** | You can buy the feature. A note such as **Also adds …** lists other features it needs, which are added to the same payment. |
| **Request access** | The feature needs an online payment gateway your business does not have yet. |
| **Request sent** | Your gateway request is in progress. Our team will contact you to set it up. |

## Buy a feature or bundle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose what to buy">
    Click **Add** next to a single feature, or **Get** (or **Upgrade to**) followed by a bundle's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay in the secure window">
    A payment window opens. If your browser blocks it, allow pop-ups for Taliup HQ and try again. Complete the payment in that window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    When the payment goes through, the page refreshes and the feature shows **Active**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Gift Cards** and **Loyalty** have their own setup. **Add** opens that setup page, where you turn the feature on for each location. See [Gift Cards](/taliup-hq/features/gift-cards) and [Loyalty](/taliup-hq/features/loyalty).

## Request a feature that needs online payments

Some features need an online payment gateway on at least one location. For those, click **Request access**, select the locations to set up in **Set up online payments**, and click **Send request**. Our team contacts you to set up the gateway. After that, you can add the feature.

## When billing needs attention

| Notice | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Your last payment didn't go through** | Your add-ons stay on for now. Click **Go to billing** and update your card. You cannot add features until the payment goes through. |
| **Your add-ons are suspended** | A payment failed and your add-ons were turned off. Click **Go to billing** to fix it before adding features. |
| **Cancellation scheduled** | Access to your add-ons ends on the date shown. |
