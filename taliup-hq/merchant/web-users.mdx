**Web users** are the people who sign in to Taliup HQ. Each has one role, and the role decides what they can do. Employees who sign in to Taliup POS are a separate list; see [Employees](/taliup-hq/merchant/employees).

## Before you start

- **View Web Users** shows the list. Inviting, editing, deleting and changing role permissions need **Manage Web Users**.
- Linking a web user to a POS employee account also needs **Manage Employees**.
- Nobody can change their own role, and the **Admin** role always has every permission.

## How to get here

In Taliup HQ, select **Web users** in the sidebar. Use **Search by name or email** and the status filter (**Active** or **Invited**).

## Invite a user

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invite form">
    Select **Invite user**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Enter the **First name**, **Last name** and **Sign-in email**, and choose their **Role**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link a POS employee (optional)">
    Under **POS employee account**, choose **None**, **Link existing employee** or **Create new employee**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Select **Send invite**. They get an email with a temporary password to sign in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The user shows as **Invited** until they accept. To send a new temporary password, open the menu on their row and select **Resend invite**; the earlier one stops working.

## Edit or delete a user

Open the menu on a user's row:

- **Edit user** changes their name, role and linked employee. Only the person can change their sign-in email.
- **Delete user** removes their access to Taliup HQ. This cannot be undone. If they are linked to an employee, you can choose **Also delete this employee**.

## Roles and permissions

Open the **Roles and permissions** tab to see what each role can do in Taliup HQ. Pick a role, then turn permissions on or off (**Find a permission**, **Turn all on**, **Turn all off**) and select **Save changes**. The **Admin** role is locked with every permission so your business cannot lock itself out.

Permission changes can take a moment to apply. See [Entity permissions](/taliup-hq/manage/permissions) for what each permission allows.
