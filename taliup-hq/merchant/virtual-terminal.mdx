The **Virtual terminal** takes card, cash and other payments from Taliup HQ, and lists the payments taken there.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Virtual Terminal** feature. Your partner sets it up for each location; see [Virtual Terminal setup](/taliup-hq/onboarding/virtual-terminal).
- Your Taliup HQ account needs **View Virtual Terminal** to see payments, **Take Virtual Terminal Payments** to take payments, add notes and send receipts, and **Refund Virtual Terminal Payments** to refund.
- Itemized sales need the **Itemized Sale** feature. Adding a client needs the **Clients** feature.

## How to get here

In Taliup HQ, open **Virtual terminal** in the sidebar.

## Take a payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the payment">
    Select **New payment** and choose the location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter what is being paid">
    **Quick sale**: enter the **Amount to charge**, and use **Add tax**, **Add discount** or **Add fee** if needed. **Itemized sale**: search for products, or add a **Custom item**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the payment method">
    **Card** charges a card. A card payment needs a client: use **Add a client** to pick one or create one. Depending on your processor, you enter the card on the page or in your processor's secure window. If asked **How are they paying?**, choose **Credit card** or **Debit card**. **Cash** and **Other** only record a payment taken another way; nothing is charged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Optionally email a receipt to the client and add notes, then take the payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Look up payments

The page shows **Payments**, **Collected**, **Refunded** and **Net** for the period. Use **Search payments or clients** and the location filter, then select a payment to open **Payment details**: the amount, method, items, client, receipt status and **History**. From there you can **Add note**, **Send receipt** or **Add client**.

## Refund a payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the refund">
    In **Payment details**, select **Refund**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to refund">
    **Full refund**, or **By item** to pick the items and fees. Enter the **Amount to refund** when asked; the page shows how much can still be refunded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a reason">
    Pick a **Reason** (or **Other** and type it), then confirm the refund.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Payments taken on Taliup POS are refunded from the device instead. See [Refund a payment](/taliup-pos/transactions/refund).
