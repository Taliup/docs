Employees are the people who sign in to Taliup POS with a username and a 6-digit passcode. The people who sign in to Taliup HQ are **Web users**, a separate list. You can link an employee to one Taliup HQ account so the same person has both.

## Before you start

- Your plan includes the **Employees** feature.
- You need the **View Employees** permission to open the list.
- Adding or editing employees and changing POS roles need **Manage Employees**. Deleting an employee needs **Delete Employees**.
- Linking an employee to a Taliup HQ account needs both **Manage Employees** and **Manage Web Users**.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, open **Employees**. When your plan also includes Timesheets, Schedule or Pay runs, **Employees** is under **Team**.

## Add an employee

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new employee">
    Click **Add employee**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the profile">
    Enter **First name** and **Last name**. **Username** is suggested from the name; the employee signs in to the POS with it. **Email (optional)** is where their schedule goes when you publish it.

    Choose a **Role**. The role decides what they can do on the POS. **Hourly rate (optional)** is used for pay runs and the labour cost on timesheets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the POS passcode">
    Under **POS passcode**, enter 6 digits in **Passcode** and again in **Type it again**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose devices">
    Under **Devices**, select the POS terminals they can sign in to. Use **Select all** or **Clear all** to change every terminal at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add employee**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A username can be used only once in your business, including usernames of deleted employees.

**Services they do** appears only when online booking assigns employees to services. Select the services clients can book this employee for.

## Edit an employee

Open the employee from the list, change what you need and click **Save changes**.

- The passcode stays as it is unless you click **Change passcode** and enter a new one.
- The **At a glance** card shows whether they are clocked in, their hours this week, their next published shift and their commission rates.

## Delete an employee

Open the employee's **More actions** menu, either on the list or on their profile, and choose **Delete employee**. They can no longer sign in to the POS. Past sales, timesheets and pay runs stay as they are.

If the employee is linked to a Taliup HQ account, the confirmation shows **Linked web user**. Select **Also delete this web user** to delete that account too. Without it, the HQ account is kept and unlinked. The checkbox appears only if you have **Manage Web Users** and the linked account is not your own.

## POS roles and permissions

Click **Roles and permissions** on the **Employees** page. Each role lists what the people with that role can do on the POS.

- Every business starts with **Owner**, **Manager** and **Employee**. These roles keep their names.
- **Owner** always has every permission, so the business owner can never be locked out of the POS. New permissions are added to it automatically.
- Roles you add can be renamed or duplicated from their **More actions** menu.
- Permission groups for features your plan does not include are hidden.

To add a role:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the role">
    Click **Add role** and enter a **Role name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a starting point">
    Under **Start with the permissions of**, choose an existing role to copy, or **Nothing** to start with every permission off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust and save">
    Turn permissions on or off, or use **Turn all on** and **Turn all off** for a group. Use **Find a permission** to search. Click **Save** with the role's name to apply the changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you remove a permission from a role, people who had that permission given to them directly lose it too.

Changes apply to everyone with the role. Taliup HQ refreshes each person's stored permissions in a background task right after you save, usually within moments. Until it runs, the POS can still use the previous permissions.

To manage employees from a terminal instead, see [Employees on Taliup POS](/taliup-pos/settings/employees).

## Link an employee to a Taliup HQ account

The **HQ access** card appears on the employee's profile only when you have both **Manage Employees** and **Manage Web Users**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the employee">
    Open the employee, or start a new one with **Add employee**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the HQ access">
    Under **HQ access**, choose one of:

    - **None**: no HQ account. Choosing it on a linked employee unlinks them.
    - **Link existing web user**: pick the account in **Select a web user**. Only accounts that are not linked to another employee are listed.
    - **Invite new web user**: enter the email address to invite and choose the account's role under **User Roles**. The email is required here, even though the field is labelled **Email (optional)**, and it cannot already belong to another Taliup HQ account. Taliup HQ emails an invitation, using the employee's first and last name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes**, or **Add employee** for a new employee.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An employee can be linked to one HQ account, and an HQ account to one employee. You can also link from the other side: in **Web users**, edit the account and use **POS employee account** to **Link existing employee**, or **Create new employee** with a username, role and 6-digit passcode.

What a link does:

- With the Timesheets feature, the linked person can **Clock in** and **Clock out** from their user menu in Taliup HQ.
- The AI assistant in Taliup POS uses the linked account's own Taliup HQ permissions for questions about HQ-only settings. Without a link, it gives general guidance only. See [AI assistant](/taliup-pos/help/assistant).
- Unlinking keeps both accounts and the employee's timesheets.
