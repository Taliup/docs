**Raw materials** are the ingredients and supplies your products use up as they sell, like flour, cheese or cups. Each has its stock and a reorder point.

## Before you start

- Raw materials are for restaurants. Your business needs the **Inventory** and **Raw Materials** features, and your Taliup HQ account the **Manage Inventory** permission.
- A raw material is measured in a unit. You can add units from the raw material form, or on the [Units](/taliup-hq/merchant/units) page when your business has the **Units** feature.

## How to get here

In Taliup HQ, open the **Inventory** group in the sidebar and select **Raw materials**. Filter by **Status** (**In stock**, **Low stock**, **Out of stock**) or **Search raw materials**.

## Add a raw material

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Select **Add raw material**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Enter the **Name** (for example Mozzarella) and choose the **Unit** it is measured in. **Per item** counts whole pieces.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the stock">
    Enter how much is **In stock**, and an optional **Reorder point**. When stock falls to the reorder point, it shows as **Low stock**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use it on products

Raw materials are used up through attribute options. On a product's page, edit one of its attributes and, for each value, choose the **Raw material** it **Uses**, the **Quantity used** and **Track stock**. See [Attributes](/taliup-hq/merchant/catalogs/attributes).

The list shows how many attribute options use each raw material, or **Not used yet**.

## Edit or delete a raw material

Open the **More** menu on its row and select **Edit** or **Delete**. Deleting cannot be undone, and the attribute options that used it stop using it up as they sell.
