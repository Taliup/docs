Four pages in the **Online store** section of **Settings** decide how your online store works: **Online ordering**, **Delivery**, **Shipping** and **Uber Direct**.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Ecommerce** feature, and your Taliup HQ account the **Manage Settings** permission.
- **Shipping** needs the **Shipping** feature, and **Uber Direct** the **Uber Direct** feature.
- Online ordering and booking follow your **Hours of operation**. See [Business details, hours and receipts](/taliup-hq/merchant/settings/business).

## Online ordering

Open **Settings** → **Online ordering**.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Take orders online** | Customers can order from your online store. |
| **Take bookings online** | Customers can book appointments from your online store. |
| **Order types** | **Pickup**, **Delivery** (**You deliver them yourself** or **Delivered by Uber Direct**), **Room service** and **Shipping**. |
| **Alert me about orders left waiting** | Emails or texts you when an order isn't accepted in time. Set **Alert after**, an **Email**, a **Phone** or both. |
| **Preparation time** | How long an order usually takes; customers see when it will be ready. |
| **Accept orders automatically** | New orders are accepted without you confirming them. |
| **Print order tickets automatically** | New orders print in the kitchen as they come in. |
| **Collapse item options** | An item's option groups start closed for customers. |
| **Half-pizza toppings** | Customers can put toppings on half a pizza (restaurants). |

Select **Save changes**.

**Shipping** stays locked under **Order types** until you add at least one shipping box and one courier pickup day.

## Delivery

Open **Settings** → **Delivery**.

- **Minimum order**: smaller orders can't be delivered. 0 means no minimum.
- **Free delivery**: turn on and set **Free on orders over**.
- **Charges by distance**: select **Add a range**, then set **From**, **To** (kilometres from your business) and the **Charge**.

With Uber Direct, Uber's quote sets the delivery charge for each order; the minimum and free delivery still apply.

## Shipping

Open **Settings** → **Shipping**.

1. Under **Boxes**, select **Add a box** and enter its **Name**, **Size (L × W × H)** in centimetres and **Maximum weight**. Taliup picks the right box for each order.
2. Under **Courier pickup**, choose the days and times the courier can pick up, within your opening hours.
3. Optionally turn on **Offer free shipping** for larger orders.
4. Turn on **Shipping** in **Online ordering**.

## Uber Direct

Open **Settings** → **Uber Direct** and select **Connect Uber Direct**. Enter the **Customer ID**, **Client ID** and **Client secret** from your Uber Direct dashboard, under Developer. Taliup checks them with Uber before saving. Customers can then choose Uber delivery at checkout. **Turn off** stops offering it and keeps your credentials.
