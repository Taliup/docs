The **Online store** group of the sidebar has four ways to bring customers to your online store: **Promo codes**, **Promotional ads**, **QR codes** and **Tags**.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Ecommerce** feature.
- **Promotional ads** and **QR codes** are in the sidebar for restaurants. Retail stores manage their ads on the **Promotional ads** tab of [Website](/taliup-hq/merchant/online-store/website).

## Promo codes

Promo codes give customers a percentage off their online order at checkout.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the code">
    Select **New promo code**. Type a **Code** of at least 6 characters, or select **Generate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the discount and dates">
    Enter the **Discount** (1 to 100 percent), then **Starts** and **Ends**. Both dates are included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Leave **On** turned on and select **Create promo code**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The list shows each code as **Active**, **Scheduled**, **Expired** or **Off**. Use **Turn off** to pause a code, or **Delete** to remove it; past orders keep their discount.

## Promotional ads

Ads are banners on your website's menu page that link to a category or product.

1. Select **Add ad**.
2. Enter a **Title** and **Description**, upload an **Image** (JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP, up to 5 MB) and choose what it **Links to**: **Category**, **Product** or nothing.
3. Pick the **Colours**, keep **Show on menu page** on, and select **Add ad**.

Turn **Show on menu page** off to hide an ad without deleting it.

## QR codes

QR codes open your online menu from a customer's phone. You can have one code of each **Type**: **Pickup**, **Dine-in** (customers pick their table, then order to it) or **Room service**.

1. Select **New QR code**, enter a **Name** (printed at the top of the card) and choose the **Type**.
2. Select **Create QR code**.
3. Use **Print card**, **Download** or **Copy link** to share it.

## Store tags

Tags describe your store so customers can find it by what you offer. On **Store tags**, pick tags from **All tags**; they appear under **Your tags**. Select **Save tags**.
