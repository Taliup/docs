**Online orders** is where orders from your online store arrive. Open it from **Today's orders** in the **Online store** group of the sidebar.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Ecommerce** feature, and your online store must be set up. See [Website](/taliup-hq/merchant/online-store/website) and [Online store settings](/taliup-hq/merchant/online-store/settings).

## The three tabs

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **New** | Orders waiting for you to accept or reject. A new order also shows an alert on every tab. |
| **In progress** | Accepted orders until they are picked up, delivered or completed. **Today** and **Scheduled** split today's orders from pickups booked for another day. |
| **History** | Past orders. Search by **Order number or customer** and filter by **Period**, **Type** and **Status**. |

Each order shows its type: **Pickup**, **Scheduled pickup**, **Delivery**, **Dine-in**, **Room service** or **Shipping**.

## Handle a new order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept or reject">
    Open the order in **New** and select **Accept**, or **Reject** and pick a reason, then **Reject order**. A rejected customer is told why and isn't charged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the ready time">
    Select **Set ready time**, adjust it, then **Send time**. The customer gets a text with the time. Use **Change ready time** if it changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move it along">
    In **In progress**, select **Mark ready**, then **Mark picked up**, **Mark delivered** or **Complete**, depending on the order type. **Back to preparing** undoes **Mark ready**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If **Accept orders automatically** is on in [Online store settings](/taliup-hq/merchant/online-store/settings), new orders skip the **New** tab.

An order marked unpaid should only be completed once the customer has paid.

## Refund or cancel an order

Select **Refund** on the order. Choose **Whole order** (refunds and cancels it) or **Some items**, pick a reason, and confirm the amount. For a shipped order, Taliup also tries to void the shipment with the carrier. An unpaid order is cancelled without a refund.

## Print

Select **Print** on an order to print its ticket.
