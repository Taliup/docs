**Website** sets up the site customers order from. Open **Website** in the **Online store** group of the sidebar, then select **View site** to see it.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Ecommerce** feature.
- Online card payments go through a location's online payment account. If no location has one, **Payment location** shows that none is set up yet; ask your ISO or partner, or [contact support](/taliup-pos/help/support).

The tabs depend on your industry: restaurants see **Store**, **Look** and **Search**; retail stores see **Store**, **Web address**, **Brand**, **Style**, **Search** and **Promotional ads**.

## Store

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Payment location** | Online payments go to this location's payment account. |
| **Menu** or **Catalog** | The items shown on the site. |
| **Web address** | Your free Taliup address. Letters, numbers and hyphens only. |
| **Design** | The site's design. |
| **Contact email** | Shown on the site for customers. |
| **Google Tag Manager ID** | Optional, for tracking visits with Google. |
| **About your business** | Shown in the site's About section. |

## Look, brand and style

- Restaurants: on **Look**, upload the **Logo**, **Banner**, **Promotion image** and **About image** (they save as soon as you upload them), write the **Banner and promotion text**, and pick the **Colours**.
- Retail stores: **Brand** holds your images and **About us** sections, **Style** the font and colours, and **Social media** the links in the footer.

## Search

On **Search**, write the **Description** and **Keywords** that show how your site appears in Google and other search results.

## Use your own domain (retail)

On **Web address**, turn on **Use my own domain**, then:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your domain and save">
    A subdomain such as `shop.yourstore.com` is the easiest to set up. A root domain needs a DNS provider that supports ANAME or ALIAS records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add two DNS records">
    Add the two records shown at your domain provider to prove you own the domain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add one more for HTTPS">
    Once the domain checks out, add the last record to turn on the secure certificate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The page shows **Domain check**, **Secure site (HTTPS)** and **Website** progress and checks again every 30 seconds. DNS changes can take a few hours to show up.
