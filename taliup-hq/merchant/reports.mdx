**Reports** builds a report for any period and keeps it in a list, so you can open, print or download it later.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Reports** feature, and your Taliup HQ account the **View Reports** permission.
- Generating a report needs **Manage Reports**. Deleting one needs **Delete Reports**. Without them you can only open reports that already exist.

## How to get here

In Taliup HQ, select **Reports** in the sidebar (under the insights group).

## Report types

| Report | What it covers | When it is offered |
|---|---|---|
| **Transactions report** | Sales, refunds, voids and authorizations by payment method, with taxes and tips | Always |
| **Orders report** | Every order with its items, discounts, fees, tax and tips | Always |
| **Items report** | What sold, how many, and its sales, item discounts and tax | Always |
| **Tips report** | Tips collected by payment type and by employee | Always |
| **Profit report** | Revenue, cost and profit for products that have a cost | Always |
| **Payouts report** | Cash paid out of the till, by type, device, employee and location | Your business has the **Payout** feature |
| **Loyalty report** | Points earned and redeemed, rewards and members | Your business has the **Loyalty** feature |
| **WEB-SRM user report** | The year's sales summary for Revenu Québec mandatory billing | Your business is set up for WEB-SRM |

## Generate a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a report">
    Select **New report**, or use **Generate a report** at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the report and its scope">
    Choose the report type. Then choose the **Location**, the **Devices** and the **Employees** it covers, or keep **All locations**, **All devices** and **All employees**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the period">
    Pick **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week**, **Last week**, **This month**, **Last month** or **Custom**, then adjust **Starts** and **Ends** if needed. The end must be on or after the start. The WEB-SRM user report always covers January 1 of this year to now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Select **Generate**. The report is built in the background and appears in **Generated reports** when it is ready. You can keep working in the meantime.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Open, print or download

In **Generated reports**, search with **Search reports** or filter the list, then:

- Select a report, or **View report**, to open it.
- Use **Print**, or the PDF icon to open the PDF.
- Use the row's **Actions** menu → **Delete** to remove a report and its PDF. You can generate it again anytime.
