The **Orders** page lists every order rung up on your Taliup POS devices or placed online, with its items, totals and payments.

## Before you start

- Your Taliup HQ account needs the **View Orders** permission.
- To change or reopen an open order, use Taliup POS. See [Find and reopen orders](/taliup-pos/orders/find-and-reopen).

## How to get here

In Taliup HQ, select **Orders** in the sidebar.

## Find an order

The list opens on **This week**. Narrow it with the controls above the list:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Dates** | **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week**, **Last week**, **This month**, **Last month** or **Custom range** (**From** / **To**, then **Apply**) |
| **Search orders** | Matches the order number, the order name or the client's name |
| **Status** | **Open**, **Complete**, **Cancelled**, **Failed to pay** or **Recurring** |
| **Location** | One location, or **All locations** |
| **Table** | One table, or **All tables** (restaurants with Table Management) |

The list shows 25 orders per page, newest first. Each row shows the order, date, client, table, device, employee, items, total and status. If nothing matches, try other dates or filters.

## Open an order

Select a row to open **Order details**:

- The order number, name and type, and the employee who rang it up.
- **Items**, with discounts per item.
- The totals: **Subtotal**, **Discounts**, **Fees**, **Taxes**, **Tip**, **Surcharge**, **Card price adjustment**, delivery fee or shipping for online orders, **Refunded** and **Total**.
- **Payments** taken on the order.

To look at a payment in detail, open it on the [Transactions](/taliup-hq/merchant/transactions) page.
