**Brand Hub** is the merchant home for store branding. The logo, favicon, and mobile background used to live on Business settings. They now live here, even before a white-label row exists.

ISO-level white-label (platform name, colors, support links) is a different page. See [UI Settings](/taliup-hq/admin/ui-settings).

## How to get here

In the merchant sidebar, open **Brand Hub**.

## Brand Identity

Helper text on the page: *Your name, logo and images, used across Taliup HQ, receipts, emails, loyalty cards and wallet passes.*

| Field | Where it is used |
|---|---|
| **Display Name** | Header and browser tab |
| **Logo** | Taliup HQ, receipts, emails, loyalty cards, and wallet passes |
| **Browser Favicon** | Browser tab |
| **Mobile Background Image** | The mobile app background. Changing it also updates loyalty wallet passes |

Click **Save Changes**. **Reset** returns the form to the last saved values.

Loyalty card pages and wallet passes pick up the Brand Hub accent colour and logo. See [Loyalty](/taliup-hq/features/loyalty#member-card-page-and-wallet-passes).
