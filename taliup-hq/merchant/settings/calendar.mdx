**Calendar settings** decide how clients book, get reminded and cancel. Open **Settings** → **Calendar** (in the **Features** section), or **More actions** → **Calendar settings** on the calendar. Select **Save changes** after you change a tab.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Calendar** feature, and your Taliup HQ account the **Manage Settings** permission.
- Online booking follows your **Hours of operation**; set them first. See [Business details, hours and receipts](/taliup-hq/merchant/settings/business).

## Notifications

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Email me about appointments** | Emails you (**Send to**) when an appointment is booked, changed or cancelled. |
| **Email reminders to clients** | How long before the appointment clients get a reminder email. |
| **Text clients a booking confirmation** | Texts the client when the appointment is booked. The client needs a phone number on their profile. |
| **Text clients appointment reminders** | Texts a **First reminder** and an optional **Second reminder** closer to the appointment. |

Every text ends with "Reply STOP to opt out"; clients who reply STOP are not texted again.

## Online booking

1. Turn on **Accept online bookings** so clients can book themselves from your booking page.
2. Under **Your booking page**, copy the **Booking link** or print the QR code.
3. Under **Get booked from Google, Instagram and Facebook**, copy the link for each channel. Each one is tracked, so you can see where your online bookings come from.

## Booking rules

- **When clients can book**: the **Minimum notice** and **How far ahead clients can book** from your booking page.
- **Staff and waitlist**: **Assign employees to services** lets clients book a specific person (availability then follows each employee's schedule), and **Let clients join a waitlist** turns on the [waitlist](/taliup-hq/merchant/appointments/waitlist).
- **On the calendar**: **Drag appointments to move them** lets staff drag a booking to a new time, day or staff member after confirming.

## Cancellations

Turn on **Clients can cancel online** to let clients cancel from the link in their confirmation email.

## Rooms and equipment

Add the rooms, chairs or machines your services need, with **How many at once** each can be used. Then pick them on each service; online booking won't offer a time when one is full.
