**Subscription and billing** shows your plans with Taliup, what you've paid and your monthly statements. Open **Settings** → **Subscription** (in the **Your business** section).

## Before you start

- Your Taliup HQ account needs the **Manage Subscriptions** permission.
- The page is there when you pay Taliup through HQ: your plan allows add-ons or has a software subscription, or you have a gift card or loyalty enrolment. Otherwise, billing questions go to your ISO or partner, or [support](/taliup-pos/help/support).
- To add features, use the [Add-ons](/taliup-hq/merchant/marketplace) page.

## The three tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Plans** | Each recurring plan with its **Price**, **Next billing** date, **Card** and status (**Active**, **Payment overdue**, **Past due**, **Suspended** or **Cancelled**). Open one for its **Add-ons** and **Payment history**. |
| **One-time payments** | Products paid once, such as equipment. |
| **Statements** | Your monthly statements with **Charges**, **Refunds** and **Net**. Select **PDF** to download one. |

Select **Export CSV** to download what the tab shows.

## Fix a declined payment

When the last payment was declined, the plan shows a note.

1. Select **Update card** and enter the new card in the secure checkout.
2. Or select **Retry payment**, then **Charge now**, to charge the card on file again.

## Cancel a plan or add-ons

- **Cancel plan** cancels the whole subscription after you confirm; **Keep plan** leaves it. Depending on the plan, it stays active until the end of the billing period.
- **Cancel add-ons** lets you pick add-ons, then **Cancel selected** and **Confirm cancellation**. Add-ons that depend on the ones you picked are cancelled too, and the page shows your new monthly total.
