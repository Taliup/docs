Three pages under **Settings** describe your business: **Business details**, **Hours of operation** and **Receipts**.

## Before you start

- Your Taliup HQ account needs the **Manage Settings** permission.
- **Receipts** is not shown for businesses on WEB-SRM, whose receipts follow Revenu Québec's rules.

## Business details

Go to **Settings** → **Business details**, change what you need, and save.

| Card | What you can change |
|---|---|
| **Business** | **Description**, a short line about your business (up to 255 characters). The **Business name**, shown on receipts and online, is changed by support: see [Contact support](/taliup-pos/help/support) |
| **Address** | Use **Find your address**, or fill in the street, city, region and postal code |
| Contact | **Phone** and **Tax ID** |
| **Charity** | The **Charity number**, for charity businesses |
| **Emails** | **Low stock emails**, to get an email when an item you track runs low. See [Inventory](/taliup-hq/merchant/inventory) |

## Hours of operation

Go to **Settings** → **Hours of operation**. Online ordering and online booking follow these hours.

1. For each day, turn on **Open** or leave it **Closed**.
2. Set **Opens** and **Closes**. A closing time after midnight shows as the next day.
3. Use **Copy … hours to every open day** to repeat one day's hours.
4. Save.

## Receipts

Go to **Settings** → **Receipts** to set what prints at the bottom of every receipt. A **Preview** shows the result as you type.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Contact email** | Shown on the receipt only; nothing is sent to it |
| **Opening hours** | A line with your hours |
| **Footer message** | A message at the bottom, such as a thank-you or return policy |
| **"Served by" line** | Prints who made the sale. Under **Show**, choose **Employee's name** or **Business name** |
| **Barcode** | Prints a barcode that looks the sale up on the register. See [Receipt barcodes](/taliup-pos/register/receipt-barcode) |

Devices pick up the changes the next time they sync with Taliup HQ.
