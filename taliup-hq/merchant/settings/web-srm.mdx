Businesses in Québec must record their sales with Revenu Québec. With WEB-SRM on, sales are validated, stored and sent to Revenu Québec. Open **Settings** → **WEB-SRM** (in the **Connections** section).

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Web-SRM** feature, and you need the **Admin** role in Taliup HQ.
- Have your five Revenu Québec details at hand: **User identification number**, **User authorization code**, **QST number**, **GST number** and **Billing file number**.

## Turn on WEB-SRM

<Warning>
  Turning on WEB-SRM creates GST (5%) and QST (9.975%), puts them on all your catalogs and removes every other tax. You can still adjust taxes afterwards.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Under **Revenu Québec**, turn on **Use WEB-SRM**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Under **Your Revenu Québec details**, fill in all five fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and confirm">
    Save your changes, then confirm **Turn on WEB-SRM?** with **Turn on WEB-SRM**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Next, on each terminal

HQ settings alone don't enrol a terminal. On each terminal, after the employee signs in:

1. On **Welcome to Taliup POS**, tap **Request a certificate**.
2. Enter a **Certificate nickname (SN)** of 8 to 32 characters.
3. For a first enrolment, tap **No — let Revenu Québec assign one**.
4. Tap **Request a certificate** and wait for **Certificate installed**.
5. Repeat on every other terminal.

See [Enrol Taliup POS with WEB-SRM](/taliup-pos/web-srm). If your terminals already take sales, follow [Start using WEB-SRM on an existing merchant](/taliup-pos/web-srm/switch-live-merchant).

## Turn off WEB-SRM

Turn off **Use WEB-SRM** and confirm **Turn off WEB-SRM?** with **Turn off**. Your Revenu Québec details are removed and sales stop being sent to Revenu Québec. Your taxes stay as they are.
