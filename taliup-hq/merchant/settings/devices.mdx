**Devices** lists your payment terminals, active or waiting to be activated, with the name and the catalog each one sells from. Taliup adds and removes devices for your business; you cannot add or delete them here.

## Before you start

- Your Taliup HQ account needs the **Manage Settings** permission.
- **Devices** appears for businesses that have payment terminals.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, open **Settings**, then **Devices** under **Payments**.

## Find a device

Use **Search devices** and the **Location**, **Status** and **Catalog** filters (**Has a catalog** or **No catalog**). Each device shows its **Type**, its status (**Active** or **Waiting to activate**) and its ID:

- **Device ID** for AiOne and Castles devices.
- **TID** for the other devices.

When Taliup POS asks for a Device ID during [activation](/taliup-pos/activate-device), enter the ID shown here.

## Change a device's name or catalog

1. Select **Manage device** on the device.
2. Enter a **Device name** to tell your devices apart.
3. Choose the **Catalog**: the items and prices this device sells.
4. Select **Save**.

The device picks up the new catalog the next time it syncs with Taliup HQ.
