The **Payments** settings page holds the payment options a merchant can manage in Taliup HQ: tips, tip adjustment, taxes on open sales and dual pricing. It also shows the surcharge your administrator set.

## Before you start

- You need the **Manage Settings** permission.
- Some sections show only when they apply to your business, as described in each section below. On a business that uses WEB-SRM, only **Tips** is shown.

## How to get here

In the sidebar, open **Settings**, then **Payments** under **Payments**.

Change what you need, then click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom of the page.

## Tips

1. Turn on **Ask for tips**. Customers pick a tip on the terminal before paying.
2. Under **Tip type**, choose **Percentage** for a percentage of the bill, or **Amount** for set amounts.
3. Under **Tip choices**, enter the three choices customers see.
4. Turn on **Tip on the amount before tax** to work tips out on the subtotal, before taxes. In Québec, Bill 72 requires tips to be worked out on the amount before tax.

To stop asking for tips, turn off **Ask for tips** and save.

On Poynt terminals, tip choices change by request once they are saved. Click **Request a change**, enter the new choices in **Request new tip choices**, and click **Send request**. The page shows when a request is in progress.

## Tip adjustment

Shown when your plan includes **Tip Adjust**. **Limit** sets the most a tip can be raised by after the payment, as a **Percentage** of the subtotal or as an **Amount**. Leave it empty to remove the limit.

## Taxes on open sales

Shown for businesses using PAX, Poynt, Castles or Ingenico terminals. Turn on **Charge taxes on open sales** and select the taxes to apply. Amounts entered on the terminal without an item get these taxes. Choose at least one tax.

## Dual pricing

Shown when your plan includes **Dual Pricing**. Card and cash prices differ by a fee. What you can change depends on how your administrator set it up:

| What the section shows | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Turned off by your administrator.** | Dual pricing is off. Only your administrator can turn it on. |
| **Fee** and **Shown as**, marked **Set by your administrator.** | Dual pricing is on with your administrator's fee. You cannot change it. |
| **Use my own fee** | Your administrator lets you set the fee. |

When **Use my own fee** is available:

1. Turn it on to replace your administrator's fee. Off, the administrator's fee applies.
2. Under **Fee**, choose **Percentage** or **Amount** and enter a value above 0.
3. Turn on **Show it as a cash discount** to present the difference as a discount for cash. Off, it is shown as a surcharge on card payments.

If your administrator has not set a fee, dual pricing has no fee until you set your own. The page then shows **Dual pricing is on without a fee. Turn on “Use my own fee” and set one.**

## Surcharge

Shown when your plan includes **Surcharge**. You can see the surcharge but not change it. The section shows **Turned off by your administrator.**, or the **Fee** your administrator set and the **Locations** it applies to. Dual pricing and surcharge are not used together on the same merchant.

## Automatic settlement

Taliup HQ has no setting for automatic settlement on this page or elsewhere in the merchant portal. To close a batch yourself, see [Close a batch](/taliup-pos/transactions/close-batch). For questions about how your batches settle, contact [support](/taliup-pos/help/support).
