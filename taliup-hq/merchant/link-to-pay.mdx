**Link to Pay** in Taliup HQ lists the payment links made on the register, and where each one stands. Links are created on Taliup POS; see [Create and send a link](/taliup-pos/link-to-pay/create-and-send).

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Link To Pay** feature. Your Taliup HQ account needs **View Invoices** to see links, **Manage Invoices** to send, remind, record payments or refund, and **Delete Invoices** to delete.

## How to get here

In Taliup HQ, open the **Get paid** group in the sidebar and select **Link to pay**.

## Find a link

The tiles at the top count and total the links that are **Scheduled**, **Awaiting payment**, **Overdue** and **Paid**. Below, use **Search by number, client or email**, the **Status** filter or the **Client** filter. Select a link, or **Open** from its menu, to see its details.

## Act on a link

Open the menu on a link's row. The options depend on its status:

| Option | When it appears |
|---|---|
| **View page** | Any link that is not a draft: opens the page the client pays on |
| **Send now** | **Scheduled** links |
| **Send again** | **Awaiting payment** links |
| **Send reminder** | **Overdue** links |
| **Record payment** | Unpaid links paid another way, for example **Cash** or **Cheque** |
| **Refund** | **Paid** links. The amount goes back to the client; card payments go back to the card. You can choose to **Put its items back in stock** |
| **Delete** | With **Delete Invoices** |

A refund here moves money. Check the amount before you confirm.
