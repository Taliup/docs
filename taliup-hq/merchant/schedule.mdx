The **Staff schedule** plans the week's shifts. Once you publish a week, staff can see their shifts, and published shifts decide who clients can book. Open **Schedule** in the **Team** group of the sidebar, or **More actions** → **Staff schedules** on the calendar.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Employees** and **Employee Scheduling** features, and your Taliup HQ account the **View Employees** permission.
- Add your employees first. See [Employees](/taliup-hq/merchant/employees). Labour cost uses each person's hourly rate.
- Only this week and next week can be scheduled; past weeks are view only.

## Add shifts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a day">
    Click an empty day for an employee, or select **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the shift">
    Choose the **Employee** and **Date**, then the **Starts** and **Ends** times, or a **Quick times** choice. An end time before the start means the shift ends the next day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat and save">
    Use **Same shift on (this week)** to add it to other days, pick a **Colour**, add a **Note (optional)**, then select **Add shift**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click a shift to change or **Delete** it. The grid shows each **Daily total** and the week's hours and labour cost.

## Publish the week

1. Select **Publish week**. A new week stays a **Draft** that staff can't see.
2. Under **Let staff know**, choose **Email everyone on this week**, **Only people whose shifts changed** or **Don't email anyone**.
3. Confirm. Changes to a published week go live right away; select **Republish** to email people again.

## More actions

- **Copy to next week** copies this week's shifts; shifts already on that week stay.
- **Copy staff link** copies the link staff open to see their shifts.
- **Print** prints the week.
