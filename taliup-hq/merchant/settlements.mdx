**Settlements** shows the card batches from your payment terminals: what is still open and what is already settled. It is a report only. You close a batch on the terminal; see [Close a batch](/taliup-pos/transactions/close-batch).

## Before you start

- **Settlements** appears in Taliup HQ for businesses whose payment terminals are PAX terminals processing with Nuvei.
- Open batches need an active payment terminal. Without one the page shows **No active terminals**.

## How to get here

In Taliup HQ, open the **Get paid** group in the sidebar and select **Settlements**. Use **Choose a terminal** at the top to switch terminals.

## Open batch

The **Open batch** tab shows the terminal's **Open batch** number and **Not settled yet**, with:

- **Net sales** (sales − refunds + tips), the number of sales, the tips and the number of refunds.
- **Transactions in this batch**, each with its status (for example **Captured**, **Authorized** or **Refunded**), time and amount.

It also shows the **Last settled batch**: when it was settled, the **Settled amount** and its totals by card type, cash and returns. Select **See all settled batches** to open the other tab.

If the terminal has nothing unsettled, the tab shows **No open batch**.

## Settled batches

The **Settled batches** tab lists settled batches with their batch number, terminal, settlement date, number of transactions and settled amount. A batch settled for a whole location shows **All terminals at this location**.

Narrow the list with **Search by batch number**, the terminal and the **Month**. The totals at the top cover everything the filters match.

## If something does not show

- **We couldn't load your settlements**: refresh the page.
- A payment is missing from the open batch: open it in [Transactions](/taliup-hq/merchant/transactions) or on the terminal.
- For questions about how batches are settled, contact [support](/taliup-pos/help/support).
