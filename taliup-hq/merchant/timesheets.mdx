**Timesheets** shows the hours employees clocked on Taliup POS. Use it to fix a missed clock-out or add a shift someone forgot to clock.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Timesheets** feature, and your Taliup HQ account the **Manage Employees** permission.
- Employees clock in and out on the device. See [Clock in and out](/taliup-pos/clock-in-out).
- Labour cost uses each employee's hourly rate, set on the employee. See [Employees](/taliup-hq/merchant/employees).

## How to get here

In Taliup HQ, open **Timesheets** in the team group of the sidebar.

## Review hours

Pick **Dates** (**This week**, **Last week**, **This month**, **Last month** or **Custom dates**) and an **Employee** or **Everyone**. The page shows:

- **Hours worked** (finished shifts only), **Labour cost**, **Shifts** and who is **Clocked in now**.
- **Shifts**: each shift with its clock-in and clock-out times, hours, labour cost and status.
- **Totals by employee**: shifts, hours, hourly rate and labour cost per employee.

A shift without an hourly rate has no labour cost. Shifts still running count once they end.

## Fix a missed clock-out

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the shift">
    Select the shift that is still **Clocked in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the clock-out">
    Choose **Now** or **At another time**, for example when they left. For a shift past midnight, pick the next day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save shift**. The page previews the hours and labour cost before you save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add or change a shift

- Select **Add shift**, choose the employee, then the **Clocked in** and **Clocked out** date and time, and select **Save shift**.
- Select any shift to edit its times, or **Delete shift** to remove it.

## Export

Select **Export CSV** to download the timesheets for the dates and employee you picked.
