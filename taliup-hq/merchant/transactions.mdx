The **Transactions** page shows every payment, refund and payout, with its history and the employee who took it.

## Before you start

- Your Taliup HQ account needs the **View Transactions** permission.
- This page is for reviewing payments. Refunds and voids of in-person payments are done in Taliup POS: see [Refund a payment](/taliup-pos/transactions/refund) and [Void a payment](/taliup-pos/transactions/void).

## How to get here

In Taliup HQ, select **Transactions** in the sidebar.

## Tabs

Each tab shows how many entries it has for the dates you picked.

| Tab | What it lists |
|---|---|
| **Orders** | Payments taken on a device for an order, including gift card payments |
| **Open sales** | Open Sale payments (a custom amount without an order) |
| **Online** | Online store, invoice, Link to Pay, hosted and recurring payments |
| **Payouts** | Cash paid out of a drawer. Shown when your business has the **Payout** feature |

## Find a payment

The page opens on **This week**. Use **Dates** to pick another period, and narrow the list with:

- **Search payments**: the processor's transaction ID, the order or invoice number, or a card's last digits. On **Payouts**, **Search payouts** matches the notes and reference.
- **Status**: **Captured**, **Authorized**, **Refunded**, **Partially refunded**, **Voided**, **Declined** or **Authorization removed**.
- **Location**: one location or **All locations**.

Amounts show what they include, for example "incl. tip" or "incl. card price adjustment". Refunds and voids show as negative amounts.

## Open a payment

Select a row to open **Payment details**: the amount and its tip, tax, surcharge and card price adjustment, the employee and **Helped by**, the card used, the card customer, and the **History** of the payment (for example authorized, captured, then refunded).

From the details you can also:

- **Open invoice**: for a payment made on an invoice, when your account has **View Invoices**.
- **View receipt** and **Send receipt**: for a donation on a charity business, to see the donation receipt or email it.
