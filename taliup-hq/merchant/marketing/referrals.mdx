**Referrals** rewards clients who bring in friends: the friend gets credit on their first visit, and the client who referred them earns credit too. Open **Referrals** in the **Marketing** group of the sidebar.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Referrals** feature, and your Taliup HQ account the **Manage Settings** permission.
- New clients are linked to the referrer when they book online through the referral link, so the program works with online booking. See [Calendar settings](/taliup-hq/merchant/settings/calendar).

## Set up the program

Under **Referral program**:

1. Turn on **Run a referral program**. Every client gets a personal link to share.
2. Set what the **New client gets** (credit for their first visit) and what the **Referrer earns** (once the friend's first visit has happened).
3. Set when the **Credit expires after**, in days.
4. Select **Save**.

## Share referral links

- Each client's **Referral link** is on their client profile; copy it and send it to them.
- To reach everyone, send a [marketing campaign](/taliup-hq/merchant/marketing/campaigns) with the referral link placeholder.

## Track referrals

**Results** shows **Referrals**, **Friends who visited**, **Credit given** and **Credit used**. **Who referred whom** lists each referral as **Waiting for first visit** or **Rewarded**. **Remove** cancels a referral: the referrer isn't rewarded and the new client's unused welcome credit is removed.

## Use credit

A client's **Credit** shows on their profile, where you can **Add credit**. Staff use it from the appointment window in the calendar with **Use credit**, then take the amount off at checkout.
