**Reviews** asks clients how their visit went after an appointment, and sends them to your Google reviews. Open **Reviews** in the **Marketing** group of the sidebar.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Reviews** feature, and your Taliup HQ account the **View Reviews** permission. Changing the request settings needs **Manage Reviews**.
- Review requests follow appointments, so they're for businesses that use the **Calendar**.

## Turn on review requests

Select **Request settings** (or **Turn on** when requests are off), then:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Ask clients for a review after their appointment** | Clients get one text or email after a visit, at most once every 30 days, only between 9 am and 9 pm. |
| **Send by** | **Text**, **Email** or **Text + email**. |
| **When to send** | How many **hours after the appointment** it goes out. Cancelled appointments are skipped. |
| **Google review link** | In your Google Business Profile, choose "Ask for reviews" and copy the link. |

Select **Save**.

## How clients answer

Clients rate their visit from 1 to 5 stars. Everyone is offered your Google link: 4 and 5 stars see it first, and lower ratings are first asked to tell you privately what went wrong.

## Read the ratings

**Ratings** shows the **Last 90 days**: **Average rating**, how many were **Asked**, **Answered** and **Went to Google**. Under **What clients said**, filter by **1–2 stars**, **3 stars** or **4–5 stars**, and select **Open client** to go to their profile.

To stop asking one client, turn off **Review requests** on their client profile. Clients can also stop requests from the link in the message.
