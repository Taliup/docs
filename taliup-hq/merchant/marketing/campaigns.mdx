**Campaigns** emails and texts your clients: one-off campaigns, birthday wishes and win-back messages. Open **Campaigns** in the **Marketing** group of the sidebar.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Clients** and **Marketing** features, and your Taliup HQ account the **Manage Settings** permission.
- Only clients who agreed to marketing get messages. Record consent on the client's profile with **Agreed to marketing emails** and **Agreed to marketing texts**, or let clients opt in when they book online. Turn these on only when the client agreed. Appointment reminders are not affected.
- **Who you can reach** shows how many clients can be emailed and texted.

## Send a campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Select **New campaign** and pick a template, or **Start from scratch**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the message">
    Enter a **Campaign name** (only you see it), choose **Send by** (**Email**, **Text** or **Email + text**), then write the email **Subject** and **Message** and the **Text message**. You can insert the client's first name, your **Booking link** or a client's **Referral link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick who gets it">
    Choose an **Audience**, or leave it to send to everyone who agreed to marketing. Clients who unsubscribed or replied STOP are always left out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test and send">
    Select **Send a test** to try it, then **Send now**, or **Schedule** and pick when to **Send on**. **Save draft** keeps it for later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your business name, address and an unsubscribe link are added to emails; texts end with your business name and "Reply STOP to opt out". After sending, the campaign shows **Sent**, **Opened**, **Clicked** and **Unsubscribed**.

## Audiences

Under **Audiences**, select **New audience**, give it a **Name** and combine filters: **Has any of these tags**, **Not seen in more than**, **Visited in the last**, **At least this many visits**, **Birthday in** and **Upcoming appointment**.

## Automations

Under **Automations**, select **Set up** on:

- **Birthday message**: sent on each client's birthday (add the **Birthday** on client profiles).
- **Win-back message**: sent to clients who haven't visited in the **Days since last visit** you set and have nothing booked.

Turn on **Send automatically**. Automations go out every day at 10 am to the clients who qualify.
