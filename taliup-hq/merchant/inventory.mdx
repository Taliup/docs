**Inventory** shows how much of each product you have, and when it is time to reorder. Stock goes down as products sell on Taliup POS.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Inventory** feature, and your Taliup HQ account the **Manage Inventory** permission.
- Reorder points need the **ReOrder Point** feature. Raw materials and units are separate pages under Inventory, with their own features.
- Each sale uses the **Stock used per sale** set on the product. See [Products](/taliup-hq/merchant/catalogs/products).

## How to get here

In Taliup HQ, select **Inventory** in the sidebar.

## Start tracking products

Select **Sync products**. Products added since the last sync start being tracked at 0 in stock. Stock you have already set does not change.

## Find products

Use the **Stock** filter (**All**, **Low stock**, **Out of stock**), **Search by name or SKU**, and **Sort** by name or stock. A product is **Out of stock** when no variant has any left, and **Low stock** when a variant is at or under its reorder point.

## Adjust stock

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the product">
    Select a product, or **Adjust stock** from its row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the numbers">
    Enter the stock for the product, or for each variant. With the ReOrder Point feature, also set the **Reorder point**: when stock falls to it, the product shows as low stock and a low-stock email is sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**. **Total in stock** adds up the variants.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Low-stock emails

A notice at the top says whether low-stock emails are on and who receives them. Select **Turn on** or **Change** to set them up.

## Update stock in bulk

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download your stock">
    Select **Update in bulk**, then **Download CSV**. It lists every product and variant with its stock and reorder point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the numbers">
    Edit the Quantity and Reorder Point columns. Do not remove columns or change the Combination IDs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the file">
    **Choose a CSV file**, then **Import**. Stock is updated for every row in the file.
  </Step>
</Steps>
