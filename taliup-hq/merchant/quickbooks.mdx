Taliup HQ sends your sales, refunds and cash payouts to QuickBooks Online as receipts, on a schedule or when you choose, and brings your QuickBooks products into HQ.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Quickbooks** feature and a QuickBooks Online account.
- **QuickBooks settings** (connecting, taxes, accounts) need the **Manage Settings** permission. The **QuickBooks** page (syncing and importing) needs **Manage Quickbooks**.

## Connect QuickBooks

Open **Settings** → **QuickBooks settings** (in the **Connections** section).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Under **Connect QuickBooks Online**, select **Connect to QuickBooks**. QuickBooks opens in a new tab: sign in, check you're connecting the right company, and select Connect. QuickBooks brings you back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your taxes">
    Under **Taxes**, check the QuickBooks tax code picked for each tax, or **Choose a tax code**. If none fits, select **Create it in QuickBooks** and choose the **Tax agency**. Once linked, a tax stays **Linked**. If **Sales tax is off in QuickBooks**, set it up in QuickBooks Online, then select **Refresh**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your accounts">
    Under **Accounts**, choose the **Deposit account** payments go into (needed for refunds; without it, sales go to Undeposited Funds) and the **Expense account** for cash payouts. Then save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Disconnect** stops syncing and removes your tax links and account choices.

## Sync sales

Open **QuickBooks** in the sidebar.

- **Sync sales**: choose **From** and **To**, check **Sales to sync**, **Net amount** and **Last synced**, then select **Sync now**. Synced sales can only be removed in QuickBooks.
- **Automatic sync**: select **Turn on** (or **Change**), turn on **Sync automatically** and choose **How often**: **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**. New sales go to QuickBooks overnight; automatic sync sends what was sold since the last sync.

QuickBooks doesn't take discounts on single items, so a discounted item is sent at its price after the discount.

If the page asks you to **Sign in to QuickBooks again**, select **Reconnect**. If it says **Finish setting up in Settings**, link your taxes or pick the missing account.

## Import products

Under **Products**, select **Import** next to **Import from QuickBooks**, pick the products and services, and import them. Items marked **In HQ** were brought in before; importing them again replaces their name, description, SKU, price and stock with QuickBooks'.
