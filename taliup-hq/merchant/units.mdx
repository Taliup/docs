**Units** are what products and raw materials are counted in, such as kilograms, millilitres or slices, and how many decimal places their quantities keep.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Inventory** and **Units** features, and your Taliup HQ account the **Manage Inventory** permission.

## How to get here

In Taliup HQ, open the **Inventory** group in the sidebar and select **Units**. Each unit shows what uses it.

## Add a unit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Select **Add unit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Enter the **Name** (for example Millilitre) and the **Abbreviation** (for example ml). The abbreviation is shown after quantities on Taliup POS and on receipts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the decimal places">
    Choose how many **Decimal places** quantities keep. The form shows an example.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**. The unit can now be chosen for products and raw materials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Built-in weight units

kg, g, lb and oz are marked **Built in, for weight barcodes**: Taliup POS reads them from weight barcodes. You can **Rename** them, but not change their abbreviation or decimal places, and you cannot delete them. See [Scale items](/taliup-hq/features/scale-items).

## Edit or delete a unit

Open the **More** menu on a unit's row and select **Edit** or **Delete**. Deleting cannot be undone. Products and raw materials that used the unit go back to per item, and products sold by weight stop being sold by weight.
