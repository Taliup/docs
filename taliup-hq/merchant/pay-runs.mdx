**Pay runs and commissions** adds up hours, commissions and tips for each pay period, so you can export them for payroll. Open **Pay runs and commissions** in the **Team** group of the sidebar.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Pay Runs and Commissions** feature, and you need the **Admin** role in Taliup HQ.
- Hourly pay comes from clocked-out shifts in [Timesheets](/taliup-hq/merchant/timesheets) and each employee's hourly rate on their profile. Shifts still clocked in don't count.

## Set commission rates

On **Commission rates**:

1. Under **Business default**, set the **Service commission** (of service sales they perform), **Retail commission** (of products they sell) and **Tips paid out** (the share of their tips paid to them).
2. Optionally add **Higher rate past a sales amount** tiers with **Add a tier**. Each rate applies only to service sales inside its band in a pay period.
3. Under **Employees**, select **Edit rates** to give someone their own rates, or **Use the default instead**. Select **Save rates**.

## Create a pay run

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the period">
    On **Pay runs**, select **New pay run** and choose the **Pay period**: **Last 2 weeks**, **Last week**, **Last month** or **Custom dates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Select **Create pay run**. It adds up timesheets, sales and tips for those dates as a **Draft**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it">
    Review **Total pay**, **Hourly pay**, **Commissions** and **Tips paid**, and **Pay by employee**. Select an employee to see how their pay adds up. After fixing a timesheet or a late refund, select **Recalculate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize and export">
    Select **Finalize**, then **Finalize pay run**. The numbers are locked and the pay run can't be deleted. Select **Export CSV** to download it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each line counts for the employee who did it, otherwise whoever helped on the sale, otherwise the cashier. Gift card sales don't earn commission.
