**Forms** holds the intake, consent and consultation forms clients fill in before their appointment. Clients get a link with their booking confirmation, and the signed copy is kept on their profile. Open **Forms** in the **Appointments** group of the sidebar.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Forms** feature, and your Taliup HQ account the **Manage Settings** permission.
- Forms are sent with services, so create your services in the catalog first.

## Create a form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Select **New form**. Under **Form details**, enter the **Form name** and an **Intro (optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add questions">
    Select **Add a question** and pick a type: **Short answer**, **Long answer**, **Single choice**, **Multiple choice**, **Date**, **Yes / No**, **Signature** or **File or photo upload**. **Heading** and **Text / consent wording** add sections and text clients read. Mark questions **Required**, and use **Show this question** to show one only after a given answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose when it is sent">
    Under **Sending**, turn on **Send with bookings**, choose **How often** (**Every appointment** or **Once per client**) and pick the **Services**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Preview** to see it as clients do, then **Save form**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you change the questions after clients filled the form in, it becomes a new version, and clients asked once sign it again at their next booking.

## Manage forms

- **View responses** (or the **Responses** tab) shows what clients answered.
- **Stop sending** pauses a form; **Start sending** resumes it.
- **Delete form** stops new bookings from getting it. Copies clients already signed stay on their profiles.

In an appointment, the **Forms** tab shows each form's status, and you can send or resend the link.
