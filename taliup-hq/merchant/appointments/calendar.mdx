The **Calendar** shows your appointments in Taliup HQ. Open **Calendar** in the **Appointments** group of the sidebar.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Calendar** feature, and your Taliup HQ account the **View Appointments** permission.
- Booking and changing appointments need **Manage Appointments**; deleting needs **Delete Appointments**.
- Services clients can book are set on each service in your catalog. Online booking and reminders are in [Calendar settings](/taliup-hq/merchant/settings/calendar).
- On Taliup POS, appointments are under Home → **Calendar**. See [Calendar](/taliup-pos/calendar).

## Find appointments

- Choose a view: **Day**, **Week**, **Month** or **List**. Use **Today** and the arrows to move around.
- Filter by **Location**, **Staff** and **Client**. Turn on **Show cancelled appointments** to see cancelled ones too.
- Each appointment shows whether it is **Confirmed** or **Not confirmed yet**.

## Book an appointment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a new booking">
    Select **New appointment**, or click a free time on the calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the client and service">
    Search the **Client** by name, or select **Create New Client**. Pick the **Service**, or give a custom appointment a **Title**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set when and with whom">
    Choose the **Location**, the **Staff** member (or **Any available**), the **Date**, **Start Time** and **End Time**, and add **Notes** if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what the client gets">
    Turn on **Enable client confirmation** to send a link to confirm, and **Enable client cancellation** to let them cancel online. If the client has a phone number, you can also text them a confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book">
    Select **Book Appointment**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change an appointment

Click an appointment to open it. The **Overview**, **Client**, **Forms** and **History** tabs show its details, the client's profile, any intake forms and everything that happened to it.

- **Edit Appointment** changes the client, service, time, staff or notes. Select **Update Appointment** to save; you can text the client the new time.
- **Send reminder** sends the client a reminder now.
- **Delete Appointment** removes it after you confirm **Delete this appointment?**; **Keep it** cancels.

When **Drag appointments to move them** is on in [Calendar settings](/taliup-hq/merchant/settings/calendar), drag a booking to a new time, day or staff member, then select **Move appointment** (or **Keep original time**).
