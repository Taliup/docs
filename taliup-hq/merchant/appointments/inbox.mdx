The **Inbox** holds text conversations with clients. Client replies to your texts (confirmations, reminders and other messages) show up here. Open **Inbox** in the **Appointments** group of the sidebar.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Inbox** feature, and your Taliup HQ account the **Manage Appointments** permission. Changing the AI receptionist's settings needs **Manage Settings**.

## Answer a conversation

1. Pick a conversation from the list, or search by name or number. Conversations marked **Needs you** are waiting for staff.
2. Type in **Write a reply…** and send it. It goes out as a text from your business number. Replying yourself pauses the AI receptionist for that conversation.
3. Select **Mark as dealt with** once it no longer needs you.

## The AI receptionist

When it is on, the **AI receptionist** answers client texts right away, day or night. Staff can read every conversation and take over at any time.

1. Turn on **Let the AI receptionist answer client texts**.
2. Under **It can**, choose whether it may **Book new appointments**, **Move appointments** and cancel appointments.
3. In **What the receptionist should know**, write your policies, directions and anything clients often ask. It only answers from your services, open times and what you write here.
4. Select **Save**.

It hands a conversation to staff (**Needs you**) when a client asks for a person, is unhappy or asks a medical question. Select **Try it** to chat with it as a client would; nothing is texted, booked, moved or cancelled.
