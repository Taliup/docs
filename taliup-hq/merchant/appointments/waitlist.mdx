The **Waitlist** lists clients waiting for a time to open up for a service. When one does, the first few matching clients are texted and emailed a link to book it. Open **Waitlist** in the **Appointments** group of the sidebar, or from **More actions** on the calendar.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Waitlist** feature, and your Taliup HQ account the **View Appointments** permission. Acting on someone's request needs **Manage Appointments**.
- Clients join from your online booking page with **Join the waitlist** when no time works, so online booking must be on.
- Turn on **Let clients join a waitlist** in [Calendar settings](/taliup-hq/merchant/settings/calendar) (**Booking rules** tab). When it is off, the page says so and offers **Turn on**.

## See who is waiting

Each request shows the client, the service, what they want (dates, time of day, staff) and a status: **Waiting**, **Alerted** or **Dates passed**. Filter by **Service**, **Staff** and **Location**, or search clients or services. Open a request to see their note, contact details and **History**.

## Act on a request

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Send next opening** | Texts and emails the client the earliest open time that fits their dates. |
| **Book for client** | Opens a booking for them on the calendar. |
| **Put back in line** | Returns an alerted client to the waitlist. |
| **Remove** | Takes the client off the waitlist; they can join again from your booking page. |
