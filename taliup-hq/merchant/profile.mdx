**Your profile** holds your own name, the email you sign in to Taliup HQ with, and your password. Everyone who signs in to Taliup HQ can change their own profile; no permission is needed.

## How to get here

In Taliup HQ, open the account menu (your name in the sidebar) and select **Your profile**. The same menu has **Language** and **Sign out**.

## Change your name

Under **Details**, edit **First name** and **Last name**, then select **Save changes**.

## Change your sign-in email

1. Under **Details**, enter the new **Sign-in email**.
2. Enter your **Current password**. It is needed to change your email or password.
3. Select **Save changes**.

## Change your password

1. Under **Password**, enter your **Current password**.
2. Enter a **New password** of at least 8 characters, and type it again in **Confirm new password**.
3. Select **Save changes**.

Leave the password fields empty to keep your current password.
