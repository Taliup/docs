**Products** lists everything you sell at the register and online. Restaurants see the same page as **Items**.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Catalogs** feature. Opening the page needs **View Catalogs**; adding and changing products need **Manage Catalogs**; deleting them needs **Delete Catalogs**.
- A product shows on a device only once it is in a catalog assigned to that device. See [Catalogs](/taliup-hq/merchant/catalogs).

## Find a product

In Taliup HQ, open **Products** in the catalog group of the sidebar. Use **Search by name or SKU**, filter by **Tag**, or **Sort** by name, price or newest first.

## Add a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Select **Add product**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the basics">
    Enter the name and **Price**, or turn on **Price set at sale** so staff enter the price on the register each time. Add the **SKU or PLU** if you scan or look it up by code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how it is sold">
    Under **Sold by**, choose **Per item** or a **Unit** and the **Stock used per sale**. Choose **Sold by weight** when the price comes from a scale barcode; it cannot be combined with **Price set at sale**. See [Scale items](/taliup-hq/features/scale-items).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add taxes and attributes">
    Tick the taxes to add. A catalog adds its own taxes when the product goes into it. Add attributes (such as size) that staff pick on the register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Add product**. Images and stock counts are set on the product page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit a product

Select a product to open its page. Change what you need, then select **Save changes** (or **Discard**):

| Section | What you can change |
|---|---|
| **Basics** | Name, description, **Price** or **Price set at sale**, **SKU or PLU**, and **Cost** (what you pay, used by the profit report) |
| **Taxes** | The taxes added when it sells, or **Tax exempt** so no tax is added, even from its catalog |
| **Attributes** | **Add attribute** to offer choices on the register; remove one from this product only |
| **Selling and stock** | Unit, stock used per sale, **Sold by weight**, and **In stock** with a link to **Adjust in Inventory** |
| **Images** | **Upload images** or **Choose from library**. The first image shows on the register and online |
| **Availability** | Turn **Available** off to show the product as sold out |
| **Organize** | The **Tag**, which groups products in reports and decides which kitchen printer they print on, and the catalogs it is **In** |
| **Shipping size** | Length, width and height, for online store shipping |

## Delete a product

Open the product's **More actions** menu (or select several in the list) and choose **Delete product**. It is removed from every catalog and category, and from the registers at their next sync. Past sales keep it.

## Import or export

Use **Import** to add many products from a spreadsheet, or **Export** to download them. See [Bulk import](/taliup-hq/merchant/catalogs/bulk-import).
