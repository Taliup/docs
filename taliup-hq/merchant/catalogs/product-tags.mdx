**Product tags** group products for reports and send them to the right kitchen station. A product has one tag.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Catalogs** feature. You need **View Catalogs** to see the list, **Manage Catalogs** to open a tag's details, create it or change its products, and **Delete Catalogs** to delete it.

## How to get here

In Taliup HQ, open the **Catalog** group in the sidebar and select **Product tags** (**Item tags** for a restaurant).

## Create a tag

1. Select **New tag**.
2. Enter a **Name** (for example Bestseller) and pick a **Colour**.
3. Select **Create tag**.

## Add or remove products

1. Open the tag. Its **Details** show the name and colour. **Products with this tag** lists the products already assigned (**Items with this tag** for restaurants).
2. Select **Add products** (**Add items** for a restaurant), tick the products, then use the **Add** button showing the number selected. A product that already has a tag moves to this one. The dialog says how many will move and from which tags.
3. To take products off, tick them and select **Remove from tag**.

You can also set a product's tag on its own page, under **Organize**. See [Products](/taliup-hq/merchant/catalogs/products).

## Rename, recolour or delete a tag

Open the tag to change its name or colour. To delete it, open its row menu in the tags list and select **Delete tag**. The tag comes off every product that carries it. The products stay.

## How tags are used

- **Kitchen printers:** on Taliup POS, a kitchen printer can receive only the items with selected tags. See [Printers](/taliup-pos/settings/printers).
