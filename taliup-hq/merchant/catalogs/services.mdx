**Services** are what clients book with you, such as a haircut or a 60-minute massage. A service has a price and a duration, and you choose where it is offered and who performs it.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Catalogs** and **Services** features. Your Taliup HQ account needs **View Catalogs** to see the list and **Manage Catalogs** to create or change a service.
- Employees must exist before you can assign them. See [Employees](/taliup-hq/merchant/employees).
- Service hours, how many appointments can run at once, and rooms or equipment only matter for online booking. When online booking is off, the form shows **Online booking is off** with a link to **Open Calendar Settings**.

## How to get here

In Taliup HQ, open the **Catalog** group in the sidebar and select **Services**. Use **Search services**, filter by **Location**, or **Sort** (for example **Shortest first**).

## Create a service

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new service">
    Select **New service**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the basics">
    Enter the **Name**, an optional **Description**, the **Price** and the **Duration**. Turn on **Price set at sale** when staff enter the price on the register each time. Turn on **Tax exempt** when no taxes should be added, even from its catalog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where and who">
    Under **Locations**, tick where the service is offered. Under **Employees**, tick who performs it (**Select All Employees** or **Clear all**). Everyone and every location is ticked to start with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set online booking details">
    With online booking on, set **Appointments at the same time**, the **Service hours** (they must fall inside your business hours; **Use business hours** copies them), **Prep time before** and **Clean-up time after**, and the **Rooms and equipment needed**. Rooms and equipment are added in Calendar Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Select **Create service**. The service opens on its own page, where you add images, attributes and taxes, as for a [product](/taliup-hq/merchant/catalogs/products).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit or delete a service

Select a service in the list to edit it. To delete one, open **More actions** on its row and select **Delete**. The service is removed from every catalog and from online booking; appointments already booked stay on the calendar.
