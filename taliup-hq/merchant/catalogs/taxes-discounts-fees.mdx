Taxes, discounts and fees each have their own page in the catalog group of the Taliup HQ sidebar.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Catalogs** feature.
- **Taxes**: opening needs **View Catalogs**, adding or changing needs **Manage Catalogs**, deleting needs **Delete Catalogs**.
- **Discounts**: needs the **Discounts** feature, and **View Discounts**, **Manage Discounts** or **Delete Discounts**.
- **Fees**: needs the **Fees** feature, and **View Fees**, **Manage Fees** or **Delete Fees**.
- Discounts and fees belong to a catalog, so make a [catalog](/taliup-hq/merchant/catalogs) first.

## Taxes

Taxes are added to products through their catalog, or one product at a time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the tax">
    On **Taxes**, select **New tax**. Enter its name (for example HST) and an optional description that staff see when picking taxes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the rate">
    Under **Rates**, choose **Percentage** or **Fixed amount** and enter the rate. Use **Add rate** only when one tax is made of several parts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and apply it">
    Select **Add tax**. Then add it to a catalog (it applies to everything in that catalog) or to single products on their page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Deleting a tax removes it from every catalog and product. When WEB-SRM (MEV) is set up, taxes come from it and cannot be changed here. When QuickBooks is connected, synced taxes are managed in QuickBooks.

## Discounts

Discounts are offers staff apply on the register.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the discount">
    On **Discounts**, select **New discount** and choose the catalog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what it applies to">
    **Order discount** (**Whole order**) or **Item discount** (**Items in a category**, then the **Category**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the amount">
    Enter an amount off or a % off, and a **Code**, the name staff pick it by (for example WELCOME10). Turn on **Take off before tax** to calculate taxes on the discounted price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save it. Staff can apply it on the register: see [Apply discounts](/taliup-pos/register/apply-discounts).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fees

Fees are charges added automatically to orders, such as a bag fee.

1. On **Fees**, select **New fee** and choose the catalog.
2. Choose **Order fee** (**Whole order**) or **Item fee** (**Items in a category**).
3. Enter the name and amount. Turn on **Add before tax** to charge taxes on the fee too.
4. Save it.

Deleting a fee stops it being added to orders. Deleting a discount means staff can no longer apply it.
