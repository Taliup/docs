**Tables** in Taliup HQ is where you lay out your dining room. Staff then see the same tables on Taliup POS. See [Tables in Taliup POS](/taliup-pos/tables).

## Before you start

- **Tables** appears for restaurants with the **Table Management** feature.
- Floor plans (tables grouped by room, shown as tabs) need the **Floor Plans** feature. Without it, all tables are on one layout.

## How to get here

In Taliup HQ, select **Tables** in the sidebar. Switch **Mode** between **Live** (change table statuses) and **Edit layout** (move and edit tables). **Full screen** gives the layout more room.

## Add a table

1. Select **Add table**.
2. Enter the **Table name**, the **Seats**, the **Floor plan** and an optional **Description**.
3. Select **Create table**.

## Arrange the layout

1. Switch to **Edit layout**.
2. Drag tables to move them. Right-click a table for **Edit table…**, **Duplicate**, **Rotate 90°**, **Make round** / **Make square** or **Move to floor plan**. Select a table to change its **Shape**, **Width**, **Height** and **Rotation**.
3. Select **Save layout** to keep your changes, or **Discard**. Tables that overlap are highlighted and must be moved apart before saving.

## Floor plans

Select **Floor plans** to manage the rooms, for example Main dining, Patio or Bar:

- **New floor plan** adds one. The order of the list is the tab order.
- **Rename**, **Disable** / **Enable**, **Move up** / **Move down** or **Delete** a plan from its actions. A disabled plan keeps its tables, but they cannot take new orders or reservations; open orders can still be finished. Every table must be free before a plan is disabled.
- Deleting a floor plan moves its tables to **Ungrouped**; the tables are not deleted.

## Change a table's status

In **Live** mode, select a table, then **Change status**: **Available**, **Occupied** or **Reserved**. Marking a table **Available** cancels its open orders and frees it. Use **Find a table** or the **Tables by status** counts to find tables quickly.

## Table order and deleting tables

- **Table order** sets the order staff see in table lists. Reordering saves right away.
- To delete a table, select it and choose **Delete table**. Only a free table can be deleted: no guests, no reservation and no open order.
