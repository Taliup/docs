Use search and filters on the Orders list to find an order, then reopen open orders in Register.

<Frame caption="Filter Orders with date range and status chips.">
  ![Filter Orders with date range and status chips](/images/taliup-pos/orders/filters.png)
</Frame>

## Search

1. On **Orders**, tap the search icon.
2. Type to filter the list.
3. Tap a row to open **Order Details**.

## Filter Orders

Tap the filter icon to open **Filter Orders**.

| Section | Options |
|---|---|
| **DATE RANGE** | **From:** / **To:** |
| **ORDER STATUS** | **All**, **Complete**, **Open** |

Use **Reset Filters** to clear. Tap **Save** to apply, or **Cancel** to discard.

There are no separate Open/Past tabs — use **ORDER STATUS** to show open orders.

## Reopen in Register

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open an open order">
    Find an order with status **Open** and open **Order Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Open In Register">
    Tap **Open In Register** to continue the sale in [Register](/taliup-pos/register).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Open In Register** and **Cancel** are available on open orders. Completed and cancelled orders do not show those bottom actions.

## Cancel an open order

1. Open **Order Details** for an **Open** order.
2. Tap **Cancel**.
3. Confirm: **Please confirm you want to cancel this open order?**
