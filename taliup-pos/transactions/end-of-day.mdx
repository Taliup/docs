**End of Day** prints a merchant documentation report for a business date. Use it to reconcile sales, payments, payouts, and top items.

<Warning>
This report does **not** close card batches or settle the terminal. It is documentation only.
</Warning>

## How to get here

Home → **Transactions** → **End Of Day**.

The button appears when the **End of Day Report** feature is on the merchant plan. Opening **Transactions** still needs the manage-transactions permission.

You can also generate this report in Taliup HQ. See [End of Day in Taliup HQ](/taliup-hq/features/end-of-day).

## Report Setup

**Devices** and **Users** default to **All devices** and **All users**. That includes every activated device and every staff user on the entity.

Tap **Change** on a row to narrow the report.

| Setting | What it includes |
|---|---|
| **Devices** | Activated terminals. Select one or more. |
| **Users** | Staff users. Select one or more. |
| **Business Date** | Today, or any day in the last 3 months |

You must keep at least one device and one user selected.

<Tip>
Leave both pickers on **All** when you want a store-wide closeout. Narrow the lists when you only need one station or one cashier.
</Tip>

## Generate and print

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the scope">
    On **Report Setup**, review **Devices**, **Users**, and **Business Date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the preview">
    Tap **Generate & Print**. Taliup POS shows **Review Merchant Report** with a receipt-style preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Tap **Generate**. The report prints on the receipt printer selected in [Printers](/taliup-pos/settings/printers).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The printed report lists the selected **Devices** and **Users** by name. Totals, payment mix, sales sources, status, top items, and who initiated payments follow.

## EOD History

Open the **EOD History** tab to reprint a report you already generated.

Each row shows the business date, total, devices, and users. Tap **Reprint** to print that saved report again.

<Note>
Older reports that were generated before this update reprint from a fresh calculation. New reports reprint the snapshot that was saved when you generated them.
</Note>

## Troubleshooting

| Situation | What to check |
|---|---|
| No **End Of Day** button | Enable **End of Day Report** on the merchant plan in Taliup HQ |
| Select at least one device or user | Keep one or more checkboxes on in each picker |
| Date not allowed | Choose today or a date within the last 3 months |
| Print fails | Printer connection in [Printers](/taliup-pos/settings/printers) |
| No history | Confirm the date range and tap **Refresh** |
