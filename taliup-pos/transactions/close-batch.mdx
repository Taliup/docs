Closing the batch settles the card payments taken since the last settlement, so the funds are sent for deposit. Once a payment is settled, it can no longer be voided or have its tip adjusted. It can only be refunded.

## Before you start

- Opening **Transactions** needs the **Manage Transactions** permission. **Close batch** also needs **Manage Settlements**. Without it, Taliup POS shows a permission message.
- Taliup POS checks your permission with Taliup HQ, so close the batch while the device is online.
- Adjust tips and capture pre-authorizations first. See [Tips](/taliup-pos/register/tips) and [Pre-authorizations](/taliup-pos/register/pre-auth).

## Close the batch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Transactions">
    From Home, tap **Transactions**. On the [Open Sale Only](/taliup-pos/register/open-sale-only) screen, tap **Options** → **View Transactions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Close batch">
    In portrait it is at the bottom of the list. In landscape it is in the app bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    What happens next depends on the payment terminal. See the table below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Payment terminal | What Close batch does |
|---|---|
| PAX and Castles terminals | Asks **Close batch?**: this settles the open card batch on this terminal, and authorizations that have not been captured are dropped. Tap **Close batch** to confirm. The terminal settles, and Taliup POS sends the result to Taliup HQ and prints a settlement report |
| Elavon card readers (Link 2500, Lane 3600) | Opens a review screen with the **Open batch totals**. It settles the open batch for every terminal on this location's account, not only this device. Tap **Close batch**, wait for **Batch settled**, then tap **Done**. A settlement report prints |
| Poynt | Opens the Poynt Settlements app. Settle there |

If there is nothing to settle on an Elavon card reader, the review screen shows **No open batch**.

## Close batch or automatic settlement

**Close batch** settles now, from this device. Some businesses also have their batch settled automatically at a set time each day. If your linked Taliup HQ account has **Manage Settings**, you can ask the assistant whether automatic settlement is enabled. Register has no control for changing it. If you are not sure how your batches settle, contact [support](/taliup-pos/help/support).

Closing the batch is not the same as the [End of Day](/taliup-pos/transactions/end-of-day) report, which summarizes sales without settling them.

## After closing

Settled payments show **Settled** in [Transaction details](/taliup-pos/transactions/details). Use the **Settlement Status** filter in [Search and filters](/taliup-pos/transactions/search-and-filters) to find payments that are not settled yet.
