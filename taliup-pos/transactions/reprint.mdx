Reprint receipts from **Transaction Details** when a copy is needed again. This path prints paper only. Email is offered at sale time — see [Customer receipt](/taliup-pos/register/customer-receipt).

<Frame caption="Print menu with Print Merchant Copy and Print Customer Copy.">
  ![Print menu with Print Merchant Copy and Print Customer Copy](/images/taliup-pos/transactions/reprint.png)
</Frame>

## Reprint from details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the payment">
    Go to Home → **Transactions** and open **Transaction Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open print options">
    Tap the print icon in the app bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a copy">
    Select **Print Merchant Copy** or **Print Customer Copy**. Payout receipts may show **Print Receipt** only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Automatic reprints

Some successful follow-up actions (void, return, post-auth, tip adjust) automatically print a merchant copy before you leave Transaction Details.

## Troubleshooting

| Situation | What to check |
|---|---|
| Print fails / connect toast | Printer connection in [Printers](/taliup-pos/settings/printers) |
| Wrong printer | Confirm **Use built-in printer as receipt printer** vs the selected **Receipt Printer** |
| Wrong copy | Choose merchant vs customer from the print menu again |
