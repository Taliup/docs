There are two ways to move value back onto a gift card, and they are not interchangeable.

| Action | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| **Void** | Undoing a gift card payment you just took, before the day is closed |
| **Refund** | A customer is returning goods and you are putting the value back on a card |

A void cancels a payment that should not have happened. A refund is a new movement of money that records the return properly.

---

## Void a gift card payment

Use this when the order was wrong, the customer changed their mind, or another payment on the same order failed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the payment">
    Go to Home → **Transactions**, find the gift card payment, and open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Void it">
    Choose **Void** and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the balance">
    The value goes back on the card straight away. [Check the balance](/taliup-pos/gift-cards/balance) if the customer wants to see it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A void puts the money back on the same card it came from. To give the value to a different card, refund instead.
</Note>

---

## Refund onto a gift card

Use this when the customer is bringing goods back.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the return">
    Open the original order or start a return as you normally would.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose gift card">
    Pick the gift card as where the refund goes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the card">
    Swipe, scan, or key the card. It can be the card they paid with or a different one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm the amount. The value goes onto the card and the receipt prints.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Which one to use

| Situation | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Rang up the wrong order, nothing has left the store | Void |
| The card paid part of an order and the rest failed | Void |
| Customer returns an item days later | Refund |
| Customer wants a refund put on a gift card instead of their bank card | Refund |
| The day has already been closed | Refund |

If you are unsure, a void is the safer choice while the payment is recent, because it undoes the original rather than adding a second record.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| No **Void** option | The payment may be too old. Refund instead |
| Void declined | Check the balance, then refund instead and tell a manager |
| Refund declined | Confirm the card is active and not deactivated |
| Voided but the customer says the balance is unchanged | Check the balance again. It is read live and is the accurate figure |
| Not sure whether the void went through | Check the balance before trying again |
