A sale paid with a gift card is refunded like any other sale, from **Transactions**. The money goes back onto the card that paid. You do not need the card in hand.

---

## Refund a gift card payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the payment">
    Go to Home → **Transactions**, find the **GIFT CARD** payment, and open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Return">
    Tap **Return**, then **Refund all** or **Refund amount** for a partial refund.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The value goes back onto the same card straight away and the refund receipt prints. The card's new balance is shown on screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A refund always returns to the card that paid. Putting the value on a different card, or refunding a cash sale onto a gift card, is not supported.
</Note>

---

## What the POS enforces

- A sale cannot be refunded for more than the gift card paid. After a full refund, **Return** is no longer offered on that payment; after a partial one, only the remainder can be refunded.
- A sale paid partly by gift card and partly another way is refunded payment by payment: refund the gift card payment onto the card, and the other payment the way it was taken.
- Refunding a sale that sold a gift card takes the customer's cash or bank card payment back; it does not unload the card. Tell a manager, who can adjust the card in Taliup HQ.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| No **Return** option | The payment was already refunded in full |
| Refund declined | Confirm the card is still active. A manager can check it in Taliup HQ |
| Customer says the balance is unchanged | [Check the balance](/taliup-pos/gift-cards/balance). It is read live and is the accurate figure |
| Not sure whether the refund went through | Open the payment again. A refunded payment is marked **Refunded** in the list |
