A gift card is a payment method, like cash or a bank card. You choose it when you take payment on an order.

## Before you start

- Gift cards must be active for your location.
- You need the card, or the code from a card the customer received by email.

## Take a gift card payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to payment">
    Ring the order up as normal and go to the payment screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose gift card">
    Pick the gift card payment option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the card">
    Swipe, scan, or key the card number. If the customer has an emailed card, scan its code or type the short code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the amount">
    Confirm how much to take from the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the sale">
    The POS applies the payment and prints the receipt. If anything is still owed, keep going with another payment method.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When the card does not cover the order

If the balance is less than the amount due, the POS takes what is on the card and leaves the rest owing. The order stays open until the balance is paid.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply the card">
    Take the gift card payment. The POS applies the available balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what is left">
    The payment screen shows the remaining amount due.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the rest">
    Take the remainder by cash, bank card, or another gift card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If a customer is unsure how much is on their card, [check the balance](/taliup-pos/gift-cards/balance) before you start the sale.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Card declined | The card may be empty or not active. Check the balance |
| Less was taken than expected | The card did not have enough. Take the rest another way |
| Emailed code not recognised | Retype the short code carefully, or scan the code again |
| Network dropped mid-payment | Retry. It will not charge the card twice |
| Customer changed their mind after paying | [Void the gift card payment](/taliup-pos/gift-cards/void-refund) |
