A gift card is a payment method, like cash or a bank card. You choose it under **Other** when you take payment on a sale.

## Before you start

- Gift cards must be active for your location. If the feature is on but the location is not activated, a note under **Other** says so instead of the button.
- You need the physical card.

## Take a gift card payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to payment">
    Ring the sale up as normal and tap **Pay**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Gift card">
    Under **Other**, tap **Gift card**. A sheet opens showing the amount due.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the card">
    Scan the card with the device's scanner or the camera icon, or key the card number, then tap **Check balance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the balance">
    The sheet shows the card's balance. If it covers less than the sale, it also shows how much will remain to pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply it">
    Tap **Apply**. The amount is taken from the card and the sale continues. The receipt prints the balance left on the card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When the card does not cover the sale

If the balance is less than the amount due, the POS takes what is on the card and the sale continues as a split payment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply the card">
    Tap **Apply** for the available balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what is left">
    The payment screen shows the amount paid this time and the remaining balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the rest">
    Tap **Next Payment** and take the remainder by cash, bank card, or another gift card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If a customer is unsure how much is on their card, [check the balance](/taliup-pos/gift-cards/balance) before you start the sale.
</Tip>

## In Open Sale Only mode

Sale mode offers the same **Gift card** button under **Other**. Enter the amount, tap **Pay**, and follow the steps above. Cards cannot be sold in this mode.

## Troubleshooting

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| No **Gift card** button | Gift cards are not activated for the location, the device is offline, or the sale contains a gift card |
| "A gift card cannot pay for a gift card" | The cart holds a gift card being sold. Take cash or a bank card for that sale |
| Card declined | The card may be empty or not active. Check the balance |
| Less was taken than expected | The card did not have enough. Take the rest another way |
| Scanning does nothing | Make sure the gift card sheet is open; a scan while it is closed is ignored during payment |
| Network dropped mid-payment | Retry. It will not charge the card twice |
| Customer changed their mind after paying | [Refund the sale onto the card](/taliup-pos/gift-cards/void-refund) |
