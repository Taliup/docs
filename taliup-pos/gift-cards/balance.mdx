A balance check tells you what is on a card. It does not take any money off it.

## Before you start

- Gift cards must be active for your location.
- You need the physical card.

## Check a balance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the balance sheet">
    On the Menu or Cart tab, tap **Options** → **Gift Cards** → **Gift card balance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the card">
    Scan the card with the device's scanner or the camera icon, or key the card number, then tap **Check balance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the balance">
    The balance appears on the sheet. Close it when the customer has seen it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The balance is read live, so it is correct even if the card was used at another one of your locations.

You can also check a balance from the payment screen: the **Gift card** sheet shows the balance before you apply the card, and you can cancel without paying.

## When to use it

- A customer asks what is left before deciding what to buy
- A card was declined and you want to know why
- You want to confirm a card loaded properly after selling it
- A customer questions what a previous purchase took off

## Troubleshooting

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Card not found | Confirm the number. A card that was never sold has no balance yet |
| Balance is zero | The card has been spent. It can be reloaded by [selling value onto it](/taliup-pos/gift-cards/sell) again |
| Balance differs from the customer's receipt | Read it again. The live balance is the accurate one |
| Card shows as inactive | It was cancelled and cannot be used. A manager can check the history in Taliup HQ |
