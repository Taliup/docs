Taliup POS can sell gift cards, take them as payment, check what is left on one, and reverse a gift card payment when an order changes.

<Note>
  Gift card support requires a Taliup POS release that includes it. If you do not see gift card options on your device, it is on an earlier version.
</Note>

---

## Before you start

- Gift cards must be **active for your location**. A manager activates them in Taliup HQ. See [Gift Cards in the merchant portal](/taliup-hq/features/gift-cards).
- Your device must be registered for gift cards. Terminals added after activation are registered automatically.
- You need the physical card in hand for most actions.

If gift card options are missing, the location is not active yet or your device is not registered. A manager can check both in Taliup HQ.

## How gift cards behave

A few things are worth knowing before you use them at a busy counter.

**Selling and redeeming are different.** Selling a card is an ordinary sale that the customer pays for with cash or a card. Redeeming is using a gift card as the payment method on someone else's order.

**A card is loaded only after the customer pays.** When you sell a gift card, the value goes on once their payment is approved, never before.

**A gift card can cover part of an order.** If the balance is less than the amount due, the POS applies what is there and leaves the rest for another payment method.

**Balances live with the processor.** The balance you see is read live when you use or check the card, so it is accurate even if the card was used at another one of your locations.

**A retry is safe.** If the network drops and you are not sure whether something went through, retrying does not load or charge a card twice.
