Selling a gift card is an ordinary sale. The customer pays for the card, and the value goes on once that payment is approved.

## Before you start

- Gift cards must be active for your location.
- You need the physical card the customer is buying.
- The customer pays with cash or a card. A gift card cannot buy another gift card.

## Sell a card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the gift card sale">
    Choose the gift card option from the register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Set the value the customer is buying.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the card">
    Swipe, scan, or key the card number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take payment">
    Collect payment the way you normally would. Nothing is loaded onto the card until this is approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand over the card and receipt">
    Once the payment is approved the card is loaded. Give the customer the card and their receipt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  No tax is charged on a gift card itself. Tax applies later, when the customer spends it.
</Note>

## If loading the card fails

If the customer's payment is approved but the card does not load, the POS tells you and offers to try again. The customer has paid, so do not send them away with an unloaded card.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Retry">
    Use the retry option. Retrying is safe and will not load the card twice or charge again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the balance">
    If you are unsure whether it worked, [check the balance](/taliup-pos/gift-cards/balance) on the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If it still fails">
    Refund the customer's payment and try a different card. Tell a manager, who can check the card in Taliup HQ.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| No gift card option | Gift cards are not active for your location, or the device is not registered |
| Card rejected | Confirm the number. A card already active cannot be sold again as new |
| Payment approved, card not loaded | Use retry, then check the balance before letting the customer leave |
| Customer wants to pay with a gift card | Not allowed. Take cash or a card instead |
