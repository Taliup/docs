Selling a gift card is an ordinary sale. The gift card goes into the cart as a line, the customer pays for it, and the value is loaded once the sale is paid.

## Before you start

- Gift cards must be active for your location.
- You need the physical card the customer is buying, or the card they want to reload.
- The customer pays with cash or a bank card. A gift card cannot buy another gift card.

## Sell a card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sell sheet">
    On the Menu or Cart tab, tap **Options** → **Gift Cards** → **Sell gift card**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the amount">
    Pick **$25**, **$50** or **$100**, or **Other amount** for anything from $5 to $500.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the card">
    Scan the card with the device's scanner or the camera icon, or key the card number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to the sale">
    Tap **Add … gift card**. A line called **Gift card ****1234** appears in the cart, with no tax. You can add other items to the same sale.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take payment">
    Collect payment with cash or a bank card. The gift card payment method is not offered for this sale. Nothing is loaded onto the card until the payment is approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand over the card and receipt">
    Once the sale is paid the card is loaded. The receipt shows **Gift card ****1234 loaded** with the amount. Give the customer the card and their receipt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  No tax is charged on a gift card itself. Tax applies later, when the customer spends it.
</Note>

### Reloading a card

Selling value onto a card that already has a balance adds to it. The receipt then shows the amount loaded and the new balance.

## If loading the card fails

The load happens right after the sale is paid. If the processor cannot be reached or refuses the card, the POS tells you and keeps the load for a retry. The customer has paid, so do not send them away with an unloaded card.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Retry">
    Tap **Options** → **Gift Cards** → **Retry gift card load**. Retrying is safe and will not load the card twice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the balance">
    If you are unsure whether it worked, [check the balance](/taliup-pos/gift-cards/balance) on the card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If it still fails">
    Refund the customer's payment from **Transactions** and try a different card. Tell a manager, who can check the card in Taliup HQ.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A receipt printed before the load answered says **Pending** on the gift card line; one printed after a failed load says **Not loaded**.

## Troubleshooting

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| No **Gift Cards** section in Options | Gift cards are not active for your location, or you are on the Open Sale keypad tab |
| Card rejected | Confirm the number. It must be a card from your own gift card stock |
| Payment approved, card not loaded | Use **Retry gift card load**, then check the balance before letting the customer leave |
| Customer wants to pay with a gift card | Not allowed for a gift card sale. Take cash or a bank card instead |
| Gift card line removed from the cart | Nothing is loaded; add it again if the customer still wants the card |
