You can attach a client to the current Register order when the **Clients** feature is available.

<Frame caption="Attach Client sheet in Register.">
  ![Attach Client sheet in Register](/images/taliup-pos/clients/attach-register.png)
</Frame>

## Attach a client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Find client">
    From the Register cart or app bar, tap the client control (person icon). The sheet title is **Find client**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find or create">
    Search by **Name, phone, or email**, or browse **Usually order around now**, **Recent clients**, and **Clients**.

    Tap a client to attach them, **No client** to skip, or **New client** to create one.

    If search has no matches, tap **Create “…”** to open **New client** with the name, email, or phone already filled in. Required fields are **First name**, **Last name**, and **Email**. Phone is optional and expects a valid US format (for example `+1XXXXXXXXXX`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    After you pick or create a client, the cart shows **Client Attached**.

    If **Show past favorites** is on in [General settings](/taliup-pos/settings/general), **Past favorites** opens next. Set quantities for items from the last 3 months, then **Continue to order**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Client Attached actions

Tap **Client Attached** on the cart to open client actions:

| Action | Label |
|---|---|
| Switch client | **Change Client** |
| Reopen favorites | **Past favorites** (when **Show past favorites** is on) |
| Remove from order | **Remove Client** → toast **Client removed from order.** |
| View details | **View** / **Tap to view client details** |

After payment, Taliup POS can email the receipt to the attached client’s address. If the client has no email, **Email** is hidden on the receipt chooser. See [Customer receipt](/taliup-pos/register/customer-receipt).
