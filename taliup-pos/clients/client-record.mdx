A client record gathers everything about one client: their contact details, loyalty membership and their history with your business.

## Before you start

- Your business needs the **Clients** feature, and your role the **View Clients** permission. Editing the record needs **Manage Clients**.
- **Transactions**, **Appointments** and **Invoices** on the record need the **Transaction History** feature.
- The loyalty card and **Points** need Loyalty to be active at this location. See [Loyalty](/taliup-pos/register/loyalty).

## Open a record

From Home, tap **Clients**, then tap a client. On a tablet in landscape, the record opens beside the list.

## What the record shows

| Part | What it shows and what you can do |
|---|---|
| Header | Name, email and phone, with **Edit** |
| **Loyalty** | **Member** with **Member since**, the points earned and redeemed, and **Send card**. A client who is not a member shows **Not a loyalty member** and **Enrol in loyalty** |
| Tabs | **Transactions**, **Points** (members only), **Appointments** and **Invoices**, each with a count |

**Edit** opens **Edit client**, with the client's details, **Address** (tap **Add address** or **Edit address** to show the fields, **Hide address** to fold them) and **Notes**. See [Create and edit](/taliup-pos/clients/create-and-edit).

**Send card** emails the client their loyalty card. It needs an email on the client: otherwise Taliup POS asks you to **Add an email on this client before the card can be sent.**

## Look at a past visit

Tap an entry in a tab to open it:

- A transaction opens **Order details**: the items, discounts, fees, taxes, tip and total, and how it was paid.
- An appointment opens **Appointment**: the date and time, the staff member and the services booked.
- An invoice opens **Invoice**: its items, totals, due date and status, and how it was sent.
- **Points** lists every loyalty change, such as points earned on a sale, redeemed, adjusted or the welcome bonus. **No points activity yet.** means nothing has happened yet.

A tab with nothing in it says no records were found for this client.
