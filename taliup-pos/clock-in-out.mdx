Employees clock in at the start of a shift and clock out at the end. Taliup HQ records each punch on the employee's timesheet.

## Before you start

- The **Timesheets** feature is on for your business. Without it, **Clock in** is greyed out.
- The device is online. Clocking in and out needs a network connection.
- Someone is signed in on the device.

## Clock in or out from Home

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the clock screen">
    On Home, tap **Clock in** at the bottom of the screen. While you are on shift, the same button reads **Clock out**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the employee">
    On **Clock In/Out**, select the employee who is clocking in or out in **Employee**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the PIN">
    That employee enters their own PIN in **PIN**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Tap **Clock in** or **Clock out**. The button shows the punch that is available for that employee. Taliup POS returns to Home.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Anyone can clock in or out on a shared device without signing out the employee who is signed in. Clocking in or out does not sign you in, and it does not change who is signed in.

## Clock in or out from your profile

1. On Home, tap your initials in the top-left corner.
2. Your profile shows **Clocked in** with the shift duration, or **Not clocked in** with your last shift.
3. Tap **Clock in** or **Clock out**, then enter your PIN on the next screen.

On Open Sale Only businesses, open your profile from **Options** → **View Profile**.

## Check your shift status

Home shows a status beside the greeting: **Clocked in** with the shift duration, or **Not clocked in**.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| **Clock in** is greyed out | The **Timesheets** feature is not on for your business. Ask your ISO or partner, or [contact support](/taliup-pos/help/support). |
| **Clocking in and out needs a network connection.** | Reconnect the device, then try again. See [Work offline](/taliup-pos/offline). |
| **Clock status is currently unavailable.** | Your profile could not load your status. Check the connection and reopen the profile. |
| Your name is not in the list | Ask an owner or manager to assign you to this device in Taliup HQ. |
