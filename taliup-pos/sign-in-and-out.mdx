Each employee signs in to Taliup POS with their own name and PIN. What you can do after signing in depends on your role's permissions in Taliup HQ.

## Before you start

- The device is activated. See [Activate a device](/taliup-pos/activate-device).
- You have an employee account on this business, assigned to this device, with a 6-digit PIN. The PIN is called **Passcode** in Taliup HQ and on the employee form.

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your name">
    Select your name in **Employee**. If the list looks out of date, tap the refresh button next to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your PIN">
    Enter your 6-digit PIN in **PIN**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Tap **Log In**. Taliup POS opens Home, or the Register if that is how the device is set up. On businesses that use Open Sale Only, it opens Open Sale.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If **Incorrect credentials** appears, check the employee and PIN, then try again.

## Switch employees

On a shared device, the next employee signs in without logging the first one out.

1. On Home, tap **Switch Employee** at the bottom of the screen.
2. Choose your name, enter your PIN and tap **Switch**.

The device continues as the new employee, with that employee's permissions.

## Log out

1. On Home, tap your initials in the top-left corner to open your profile.
2. Tap **Log Out**, then **Log Out** again to confirm.

The sign-in screen opens.

<Note>
  The **Log Out** icon in the top-right corner of Home does not sign you out. It only sends Taliup POS to the background. Use the profile screen to sign out.
</Note>

## When Taliup POS signs you out on its own

Except on Poynt terminals, Taliup POS signs the current employee out when:

- The screen turns off.
- The device shuts down or restarts.
- The app stays in the background for 2 hours.

On Poynt terminals, the employee stays signed in in these cases.

## Sign in without a connection

You can sign in while offline if this device has completed at least one online sign-in. See [Work offline](/taliup-pos/offline).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| Your name is not in the list | Ask an owner or manager to assign you to this device in Taliup HQ. See [Employees](/taliup-hq/merchant/employees). |
| **Incorrect credentials** | Re-enter the PIN. If you forgot it, an owner can set a new one. See [Manage employees](/taliup-pos/settings/employees). |
| **New update (v…) is available!** | A newer version is ready. Install it when convenient. |
| An update is required | Install the required version before you continue. |
