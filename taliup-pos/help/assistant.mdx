The AI assistant answers questions about using Taliup POS and Taliup HQ, such as "How do I refund a sale?" or "Why can't I see the Tables tile?". It answers from these docs and from a short summary of how your business, this device and your employee access are set up.

## Before you start

- **AI Assistant** is turned on for your business in Taliup HQ. It is not offered in the Marketplace. Ask your ISO or partner, or [contact support](/taliup-pos/help/support).
- The device is online.

When AI Assistant is off, Taliup POS does not show the assistant anywhere.

## Open the assistant

Open it from any of these places:

- **Home**: tap the **AI Assistant** icon in the top bar, next to **Log Out**.
- **Register**: tap **Order actions** (the menu icon in the app bar), then **AI Assistant** at the end of the **Device** section.
- **Open Sale Only**: tap **Options**, then **AI Assistant** in **More Options**.

The assistant opens full screen. Tap **Back** to return. A sale in progress on the Register stays in the cart.

## Ask a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type your question">
    Type up to 2,000 characters in the box at the bottom. To send a suggested question immediately, tap it and skip the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Tap the send button. A thinking indicator shows while the answer is prepared. Some answers take longer than others.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the answer">
    The answer appears with the titles of the help articles it used. You can open those articles on [docs.taliuphq.com](https://docs.taliuphq.com/). They do not open on the device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Ask in English, French or Spanish. The assistant answers in the language of your question.

To start over, tap **New conversation** in the top bar.

## What the assistant can and cannot do

The assistant explains how to do things. It cannot do them for you.

- It does not change settings, send messages or emails, or create, change or delete anything.
- It cannot see your sales, orders, clients, items or reports.
- Payments, refunds, voids, closing a batch, card reader operations and WEB-SRM operations are always done by you, in Taliup POS or Taliup HQ. The assistant can explain the steps.
- You can ask how a feature works even when it is off or you lack a permission. The assistant explains the public steps and who can help you get access.
- If it is not sure, it says so and points you to the docs or to support.

## When a conversation is cleared

Taliup POS shows only the current conversation. It starts a new, empty conversation when you tap **New conversation**, sign in (including after **Switch Employee**), log out, or when the app restarts. On devices that sign you out when the screen turns off, that clears it too.

## Offline and errors

When your sign-in is valid and the device is offline, **You're offline. Connect to the internet to ask a question.** appears above the message box. You can keep using the rest of Taliup POS.

If you signed in offline without a current online session, or Taliup HQ rejects your sign-in after an employee switch, the assistant shows **Sign in again while online to use the assistant.** Reconnect the device and sign in again. If an answer cannot be prepared, the message shows why. Use **Retry** when it is offered.

If your business reaches its monthly assistant limit, the assistant tells you, and you can ask again next month.

## Privacy

- Your messages and a limited summary of your setup are processed by Claude, an AI model, through Amazon Bedrock in Canada. The summary does not include names of your business, locations, employees or clients.
- Taliup HQ keeps conversations for traceability, support and quality improvement.
- Each day, Taliup HQ deletes conversation content older than 90 days from its live database. Earlier database backups may still contain those messages until the backups expire.
- Do not enter card numbers, passwords, PINs or personal information that the question does not need.
