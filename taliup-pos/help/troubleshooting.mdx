## Signing in

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| **Enter your Device ID to activate** appears | The device is not activated yet. See [Activate a device](/taliup-pos/activate-device). |
| Your name is not in the **Employee** list | Ask an owner or manager to assign you to this device in Taliup HQ. See [Employees in Taliup HQ](/taliup-hq/merchant/employees). |
| **Incorrect credentials** | Check the employee and PIN. An owner can set a new PIN in [Manage employees](/taliup-pos/settings/employees). |
| **This terminal has not been online yet, so it cannot verify your passcode.** | Connect the device to the internet and sign in once online. See [Work offline](/taliup-pos/offline). |
| You were signed out | Taliup POS signs you out when the screen turns off or the app sits in the background for 2 hours, except on Poynt. See [Sign in and out](/taliup-pos/sign-in-and-out). |
| **Terms & Conditions** or **Software Subscription Required** appears | The business must finish setup first. See [Activate a device](/taliup-pos/activate-device). |

## A feature or option is missing

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| A tile or option you expected is not shown | Some features appear only when they are on for your business, or only for some industries. Ask your ISO or partner, or [contact support](/taliup-pos/help/support). |
| **This feature is not enabled on this account.** | The feature is off for your business. An HQ user can add some features from **Settings** → **Marketplace** in Taliup HQ. |
| **You do not have permission to access …** or a similar message | Your role lacks the permission. See [Permissions](/taliup-pos/permissions) and ask an owner or manager. |
| **Only an owner can manage employees.** | Employee management needs the **Owner** role. See [Manage employees](/taliup-pos/settings/employees). |
| **Clock in** is greyed out | The **Timesheets** feature is off. See [Clock in and out](/taliup-pos/clock-in-out). |

## Connection

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| Screens don't load or show a network error | Check the device's internet connection. See [Work offline](/taliup-pos/offline) for what works without one. |
| Sales made offline are not in Taliup HQ | Go to Home → **Settings** → **Tools** → **Taliup HQ sync** and tap **Sync now**. |
| **Server is currently Unavailable.** | Taliup HQ could not be reached. Wait a moment and try again. |
| A red **Offline Mode** banner (WEB-SRM devices) | See [Work offline with WEB-SRM](/taliup-pos/web-srm/offline). |

## Card reader

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| **Card reader disconnected** | Check the reader's cable, power or network, then tap **Reconnect**. |
| Wrong reader selected | Change it in [Device Settings](/taliup-pos/settings/device). |
| **Companion display disconnected** | Make sure the companion app is open on the customer display. Wait for it to reconnect, or tap **Connection Diagnosis**. |

## Receipts and printers

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| Receipts or kitchen tickets don't print | Check the printer is on, has paper and is connected, then review [Printers](/taliup-pos/settings/printers). |

## Updates

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| **New update (v…) is available!** | Install the newer version when convenient. |
| An update is required | Install the required version before you continue. |

To find your version, see the bottom of **Settings**.
