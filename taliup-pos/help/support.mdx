Most questions about Taliup POS and Taliup HQ are answered in these docs. When you need a person, contact support.

## Search the docs

Go to [docs.taliuphq.com](https://docs.taliuphq.com/) and use the search bar, or start from [Taliup POS](/taliup-pos) or [Troubleshooting](/taliup-pos/help/troubleshooting).

If the [AI assistant](/taliup-pos/help/assistant) is turned on for your business, you can also ask it from Taliup POS.

## Support contacts

[tech@taliup.ca](mailto:tech@taliup.ca) · +1 800-468-4798

## If you work with an ISO or partner

Many businesses get Taliup through an ISO or partner. Your ISO or partner may provide its own support channel. If it does, use that channel instead of Taliup support.

## What to have ready

Support can help you faster when you have:

- Your business name.
- The **Device ID** of the device with the problem. It is shown in Taliup HQ under **Settings** → **Devices**.
- The Taliup POS version, shown at the bottom of **Settings** on the device.
- What you were doing, the exact message on screen, and the time it happened.

<Warning>
  Never send full card numbers, card security codes, passwords or PINs to support.
</Warning>
