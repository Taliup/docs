A member is one of your clients. Enrolling takes a few seconds and needs nothing more than a phone number or email address.

## Before you start

- Loyalty must be active for your location.
- Ask the customer for the phone number or email they want on the account.

## Enrol a customer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Loyalty">
    From an open order or the register menu, choose **Loyalty**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for the customer">
    Type their phone number, email, or name. Matching clients appear with their membership state.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them as a client if needed">
    If nobody matches, create the client with their name and phone or email, then search again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Enrol">
    Tap **Enrol** next to the client. The POS confirms the account with the processor and shows the new member with a zero balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach to the order">
    If you started from an order, the member is attached to it so this sale earns points.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enrol with a physical card

If your business hands out loyalty cards, enrol the same way and enter or scan the card number when asked. The card becomes the member's account. A card can belong to only one member.

<Tip>
  Cardless is the default. Members do not need to carry anything; you find them by phone or email every time.
</Tip>

## Already a member?

Searching for a customer who is already enrolled shows their balance instead of an **Enrol** button. Tapping them attaches the member to the current order.

## Troubleshooting

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| No Enrol button | Loyalty is not active for your location, or the customer is already a member |
| Customer not found | Search matches phone digits, email, or name from your client list. Add them as a client first |
| "No loyalty card numbers are available" | The business has run out of account numbers. Ask a manager to add some in Taliup HQ |
| "This card is already assigned to another member" | The physical card belongs to someone else. Enrol without a card, or use a different card |
| "Card could not be verified" | The card is not one of this business's loyalty cards. Enrol without a card |
