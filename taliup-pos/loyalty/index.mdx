</Note>

Loyalty rewards your regular customers with points. They earn points on what they buy and spend those points as a discount on a later order. No card is needed: you find a member by phone number, email, or name.

## Before you start

- Loyalty must be **active for your location**. A manager activates it in Taliup HQ. See [Loyalty in the merchant portal](/taliup-hq/features/loyalty).
- Your device must be registered for loyalty. Terminals added after activation are registered automatically.
- The customer must be a **client**. You can add them on the spot when you enrol them.

If loyalty options are missing, the location is not active yet or your device is not registered. A manager can check both in Taliup HQ.

## What you can do

| Task | When |
| --- | --- |
| [Enrol a customer](/taliup-pos/loyalty/enroll) | The first time a customer wants to join |
| [Earn points](/taliup-pos/loyalty/earn) | On every sale, by attaching the member to the order before you take payment |
| [Redeem points](/taliup-pos/loyalty/redeem) | When a member wants to use their points on an order |
| Check a balance | Any time, by tapping the attached member on the order |

## How points work

**Points are earned on the goods, not the tax.** Your manager sets how many points each dollar earns. Points are computed on the order subtotal after discounts and before tax, so tips and taxes never earn points.

**Rewards come in blocks.** For example, every 100 points might be worth 5.00. A member with 250 points can redeem 100 or 200 points, not 250.

**A reward is a discount, not a payment.** Redeeming points lowers the order subtotal before tax. The customer still pays the rest with cash, a bank card, or a gift card.

**Points work at all of this business's locations.** A member who earns at one store can redeem at another. Points never work at a different business.

**Balances live with the processor.** The balance you see is read live when you check it, so it is accurate even if the member was just served at another location.
