Points are awarded automatically when an order with a member attached is paid. Your only job is to attach the member before taking payment.

## Before you start

- Loyalty must be active for your location.
- The customer must be enrolled. If not, [enrol them](/taliup-pos/loyalty/enroll) first.

## Attach a member to an order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ring up the order">
    Add items as normal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Loyalty">
    Choose **Loyalty** on the order and search for the member by phone, email, or name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the member">
    Their balance is shown and the member is attached to the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take payment">
    Finish the sale with any payment method. When payment is approved, the POS awards the points and prints the new balance on the receipt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How many points

The manager sets the rate in Taliup HQ. Points are computed on the **subtotal after discounts and before tax**, then rounded per the program. Tips, fees, and taxes never earn points. A sale below the program's minimum purchase earns nothing.

If a reward was redeemed on the same order, points are earned on the subtotal **after** the reward discount.

## When the order is voided

Voiding an order that earned points takes those points back automatically. If the member has already spent some of them, the void is refused by the processor because a balance cannot go below zero. Tell a manager so they can adjust the member.

## Troubleshooting

| Situation | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| No points on the receipt | The member was not attached before payment, or the sale was below the minimum purchase |
| Fewer points than expected | Points are based on the pre-tax subtotal after discounts and rounded per the program |
| "Phone already taken" noted on the member | The processor could not attach the customer's phone to the account. Points are still awarded. Ask a manager to check the phone on the client record |
| Network dropped after payment | Retry from the order. Points are never awarded twice for the same sale |
