**Printer Settings** manages Bluetooth printers, the receipt printer, and kitchen printer routing.

<Frame caption="Printer Settings with receipt and kitchen printer sections.">
  ![Printer Settings with receipt and kitchen printer sections](/images/taliup-pos/settings/printers.png)
</Frame>

## How to get here

Home → **Settings** → **General** → **Printer Settings**.

## Manage printers

Under **Printers** / **Manage your Bluetooth printers**:

1. Tap **Manage Printers** → **My Printers**.
2. Use **Add Paired Printer** to add a paired device.
3. **Rename** or **Remove** printers as needed.

## Receipt printer

| Control | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Receipt Printer** | Select which printer prints receipts |
| **Use built-in printer as receipt printer** | Use the terminal’s built-in printer when available |

## Kitchen printers

Kitchen printers route tickets by product tags.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a kitchen printer">
    Tap **Add printer** to open **Add kitchen printer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure">
    Set **Name**, choose a **Paired printer**, optionally select **Product tags**, and decide whether to **Print non-tagged items to this printer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save**. Edit later with **Manage**, or **Remove** a kitchen config.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each kitchen config shows the paired printer and tag summary (**Tags: none (default routing)** or a selected count).
