Owners can add and update the business's employees directly from Taliup POS. For roles, permissions and the devices each employee can use, use Taliup HQ. See [Employees in Taliup HQ](/taliup-hq/merchant/employees).

## Before you start

- You are signed in with the **Owner** role. Other roles see **Only an owner can manage employees.**
- Your role has the permission for the task: **View Employees** to see the list, **Manage Employees** to add or edit, **Delete Employees** to delete. See [Permissions](/taliup-pos/permissions).
- The device is online.

## Open Manage Employees

Go to Home → **Settings** → **Tools** → **Manage Employees**. See [Settings](/taliup-pos/settings) for how sections appear in portrait and landscape.

The list shows each employee. Use **Search** to find one. Long lists have **Previous** and **Next**.

## Add an employee

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Tap **Add employee**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Fill in **First Name**, **Last Name** and **Username**. **Email (optional)** and **Hourly rate (optional)** can stay empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the PIN">
    Enter a 6-digit PIN in **Passcode (6 digits)** and again in **Confirm passcode**. The employee uses it to sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Add employee**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit an employee or reset a PIN

1. Tap the employee in the list.
2. Change the details. To set a new PIN, enter it in **New passcode (optional)** and **Confirm passcode**. Leave both empty to keep the current PIN.
3. Tap **Save changes**.

## Delete an employee

1. Tap the trash icon on the employee's row.
2. Tap **Delete employee** to confirm. This cannot be undone.

You cannot delete yourself or an employee with the **Owner** role.

## WEB-SRM devices

On devices that bill with WEB-SRM in Québec, each employee also needs to be registered with Revenu Québec. See [Register employees with WEB-SRM](/taliup-pos/web-srm/employees).
