Discounts in Register apply to the whole order. You can pick one or more preset discounts from your catalog, or enter a custom one.

## Before you start

- The **Discounts** feature must be on for your business. Without it, Taliup POS explains that the feature is not available instead of opening the sheet.
- Discounts are added on the **Menu** or **Catalog** tab, so this device needs a catalog. They are not offered on the **Open Sale** tab, on table orders, or on the [Open Sale Only](/taliup-pos/register/open-sale-only) screen.
- Preset discounts come from the catalog assigned to this device. Seeing them needs the **View Discounts** permission. Without it, the **Preset** list is empty and you can still add a **Custom** discount.
- Add at least one item first. Otherwise Taliup POS shows **You must add an item first.**

## Add a discount

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Order actions">
    In Register, tap **Order actions** (the menu icon in the app bar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Add Order Discounts">
    In the **Adjust** section, tap **Add Order Discounts**. The **Add discount** sheet opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick presets or enter a custom discount">
    - **Preset** — tap each discount to apply. Each row shows its value and whether it is **Pre-Tax** or **Post-Tax**. Tap a selected row again to remove it.
    - **Custom** — choose **Percentage** or **Fixed**, choose **Pre-Tax** or **Post-Tax**, then type the value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    The button shows how much the total changes, for example **Add −$5.00**. Tap it to apply the discount.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A **Pre-Tax** discount reduces the amount that is taxed. A **Post-Tax** discount comes off after tax.

The discount cannot bring the total below zero. If it would, Taliup POS asks you to adjust the amount.

## Remove a discount

Discounts appear in the [cart](/taliup-pos/register/cart) under the items, each as a **Discount** row. Tap the delete control on the row to remove it.

If you remove or change an item and the discount would then be larger than the order, Taliup POS warns you that the order-level discounts will be removed too.

## Other discounts on an order

- **Loyalty reward** — redeeming loyalty points adds a **Loyalty reward** discount. See [Loyalty](/taliup-pos/register/loyalty).
- **Fees** — **Add Order Fees**, in the same **Adjust** section, works the same way for fees. It needs the **Fees** feature.
