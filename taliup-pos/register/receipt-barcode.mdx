When **Receipt barcode** is on, paper and emailed receipts include a Code 128 barcode. Scan it on the terminal, or type the token in **Orders** or **Transactions** search, to open that sale.

The setting is off by default.

## Turn it on

In Taliup HQ, open **Settings** → **Receipts**. Turn on **Receipt barcode**, then **Save changes**. The receipt preview on that page shows where the barcode will print.

## Scan a receipt

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan the barcode">
    From Register, scan the barcode with the built-in scanner or **Open Scanner**. A wedge scanner works the same way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the sale">
    Taliup POS looks up the token and opens the matching order or transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On **Open Sale Only**, a receipt that belongs to a catalog or table order cannot be opened. Scan it from a terminal that has **Orders**.

If Taliup HQ cannot be reached, Taliup POS shows **Couldn't look up the receipt. Check the connection and try again.**

## Search by token

On **Orders** or **Transactions**, tap search and type the token printed under the barcode. The list matches that sale, including payments that have no order.

## Where it prints

The barcode prints on customer and merchant copies after a sale, and on void and refund receipts printed from Transaction Details. The emailed receipt includes it too.
