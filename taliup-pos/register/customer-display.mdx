On a terminal with a customer-facing screen, Taliup POS shows the customer their order and asks them the questions that are theirs to answer. There is nothing to turn on: Taliup POS uses the customer screen automatically when the device has one.

## Before you start

- Taliup POS uses the screen that Android reports as the device's customer (presentation) display. Poynt terminals use their own customer display.
- Setups where the card payment runs in **Taliup Companion** on a separate PAX terminal (for example Elys) work differently: the customer sees the Companion terminal. See [Device settings](/taliup-pos/settings/device).

## What the customer sees

| When | On the customer display |
|---|---|
| No order in progress | Your business name with the logo |
| While you add items | The items, the number of items and the total, with the tax, tip and card fee included when they apply |
| Tip prompt | **Add a Tip** with your tip presets, **Custom** and **No Tip**, then **Confirm**. See [Tips](/taliup-pos/register/tips) |
| Dual pricing | **Confirm your payment type**: the **Cash** and **Card** prices, to choose from |
| Credit card surcharge | **REVIEW & CONFIRM (CREDIT)** with the subtotal, tax, tip, **Credit Card Surcharge** and **Total To Be Charged**. **Proceed** accepts; **Prefer Debit?** / **Pay With Debit** switches to debit when it is offered |
| After payment | **Choose customer receipt**: **No Receipt**, **Paper**, and **Email** when available. See [Customer receipt](/taliup-pos/register/customer-receipt) |

While the customer chooses a receipt, your screen shows **Customer is choosing a receipt…**, with **Take over** to choose for them.

## If the customer display stays blank or shows the wrong thing

- Check that the customer screen is connected and turned on, then restart Taliup POS.
- On Poynt, the customer display belongs to the Poynt terminal; restart the terminal if it stops updating.
- Still blank: see [Troubleshooting](/taliup-pos/help/troubleshooting) or contact [support](/taliup-pos/help/support).
