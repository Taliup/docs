A pre-authorization holds an amount on the customer's card without charging it. You capture it later with **Post Auth**, or release it.

## Before you start

- Creating a pre-authorization needs the **Pre-Authorization** permission. Capturing one needs **Post-Authorization**. Voiding one needs **Manage Transactions**.
- Pre-authorizations are not available on WEB-SRM (Revenu Québec) registers. **Create Auth** is not shown there.
- A pre-authorization is for a typed amount, not catalog items.

## Create a pre-authorization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Create Auth">
    In Register, go to the **Open Sale** tab, tap **Order actions** (the menu icon), then **Create Auth** in the **Bill** section. On the [Open Sale Only](/taliup-pos/register/open-sale-only) screen, tap **Options** → **Create Auth**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Auth mode">
    On **Switch to Auth Mode**, tap **Enter Auth Mode**. If the cart has items, they are cleared. An **Auth** banner shows you are in Auth mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Type the **Auth amount** on the keypad.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the card key">
    Tap **Card** or **Credit**, or **Manual entry** on Elavon card readers. If your business uses dual pricing or a credit card surcharge, the customer confirms it. There is no tip prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Present the card">
    Follow the card reader. The approved pre-authorization appears in [Transactions](/taliup-pos/transactions) as **Authorized**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To return to normal sales, tap **Exit** in the **Auth** banner, then **Enter Payment Mode**.

If **Create Auth** is greyed out, tapping it shows **You do not have permission to create pre-authorizations.**

## Capture a pre-authorization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the authorization">
    Go to Home → **Transactions** and tap the **Authorized** payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Post Auth">
    **Capture this authorization?** shows the amount and card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Tap **Capture [amount]**. The amount is charged and included in the next batch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Release a pre-authorization

From the authorization's details, tap **Void Auth** and confirm **Void [amount]**. On Elavon card readers (Link 2500, Lane 3600) the button is **Delete Auth** instead, confirmed with **Delete [amount]**. Neither can be undone.

## Things to know

- **Post Auth**, **Void Auth** and **Delete Auth** work only on the terminal that took the pre-authorization. On another device, Taliup POS tells you which terminal to use.
- Capture before you close the batch. On PAX and Castles terminals, [closing the batch](/taliup-pos/transactions/close-batch) drops authorizations that have not been captured.
- On Poynt, the available actions come from the live status on the Poynt terminal. If it cannot be read, the details show **Live Poynt status unavailable.**
