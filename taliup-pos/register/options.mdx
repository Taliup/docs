Tap **Order actions** (the menu icon in the Register app bar) to open the menu. In portrait the options are listed; in landscape each section is a column. Sections with nothing to show are hidden.

What you see depends on the Register tab (**Open Sale** or **Menu** / **Catalog**), whether the order belongs to a table, your role's permissions and your business's features. A red dot on the icon means something needs attention, such as a gift card that was not loaded.

## Bill

| Option | What it does | When it appears |
|---|---|---|
| **Print Entire Bill** | Prints the bill for the whole order | Not on the **Open Sale** tab |
| **Print individual bills** | Prints a bill per guest | Table orders |
| **Create Auth** | Switches to Auth mode to pre-authorize a card. See [Pre-authorizations](/taliup-pos/register/pre-auth) | **Open Sale** tab, not a table order, not on WEB-SRM registers. Greyed out without **Pre-Authorization** |
| **Create Refund** | Switches to Refund mode to refund an amount without an original sale. See [Refund without a sale](/taliup-pos/register/non-referenced-refund) | **Open Sale** tab, not a table order. Greyed out without **Non-Ref Refunds** |
| **Create Cash Payout** | Pays cash out of the drawer. See [Cash payouts](/taliup-pos/register/cash-payouts) | When the **Payout** feature is on, or to show how to get it. Not on WEB-SRM registers without it |

In Auth or Refund mode, a banner shows **Auth** or **Refund**. Tap **Exit** in the banner to return to normal sales.

## Gift Cards

Shown when gift cards are activated for this location. Every option needs a connection. See [Gift cards](/taliup-pos/register/gift-cards).

| Option | When it appears |
|---|---|
| **Sell gift card** | Not on the **Open Sale** tab, not a table order |
| **Reload gift card** | Not on the **Open Sale** tab, not a table order |
| **Gift card balance** | Always |
| **Retry gift card load** | When a paid card could not be loaded. Shows how many are pending |

## Adjust

| Option | What it does | When it appears |
|---|---|---|
| **Add Order Discounts** | Adds preset or custom discounts. See [Apply discounts](/taliup-pos/register/apply-discounts) | Not on the **Open Sale** tab, not a table order. Needs the **Discounts** feature |
| **Add Order Fees** | Adds preset or custom fees | Not on the **Open Sale** tab, not a table order. Needs the **Fees** feature |
| **Add Custom Item** | Adds an item with a name and price you type | Not on the **Open Sale** tab |
| **Note** | Adds a note to the order | Always |
| **Helped by** | Credits the sale to the employee who served the customer. See [Helped by](/taliup-pos/register/helped-by) | Not on the **Open Sale** tab |
| **Add Custom Order Name** | Names the order | Restaurants, not on the **Open Sale** tab |
| **Table #** and **# of Customers** | Records the table number and party size | WEB-SRM counter orders when Table Management is off |

## Order

| Option | What it does | When it appears |
|---|---|---|
| **Send To Kitchen** | Sends items to kitchen printers | Restaurants, not on the **Open Sale** tab |
| **Save Order** | Saves the order to finish later | Not on the **Open Sale** tab. Greyed out while the order loads a gift card or uses a loyalty reward: pay those sales now |
| **Cancel Order** | Cancels the order after you confirm | Not on the **Open Sale** tab |
| **Tables** | Opens the floor plan | Table Management is on |
| **Complete Order** | Completes a table order after you confirm | Table orders |
| **Move Order** | Moves the order to another table | Table orders with Table Management on |
| **Combine Items** | Combines this table's orders | Table orders with Table Management on |
| **Increase Party Size** | Adds guests to the table | Table orders with Table Management on |

## Device

| Option | When it appears |
|---|---|
| **Open Cash Drawer** | Always |
| **Open Scanner** | Not on the **Open Sale** tab. Also used for [scale barcodes](/taliup-pos/register/scale-barcodes) |
| **AI Assistant** | Your business has the **AI Assistant** feature. See [AI assistant](/taliup-pos/help/assistant) |

## Correcting a WEB-SRM order

While you correct a paid order on a WEB-SRM register, the menu shows only **Adjust** and **Device**, plus **Cancel correction** under **Correct Order**. See [Cancel and correct](/taliup-pos/web-srm/cancel-and-correct).

## If an option is missing

- Switch from the **Open Sale** tab to **Menu** or **Catalog**: most order tools are hidden on **Open Sale**.
- Greyed-out options explain why when you tap them, for example a missing permission. Ask a manager to change your role in Taliup HQ.
- Features your business does not have show what they need instead of opening.
- On the [Open Sale Only](/taliup-pos/register/open-sale-only) screen, the **Options** menu has its own, shorter list.
