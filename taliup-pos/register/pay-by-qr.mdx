With Pay by QR, the bill carries a QR code. The customer scans it, adds a tip, and pays what is still owed from their phone. The payment goes on the same order, and Taliup POS closes the order once it is paid.

## Turn it on

A manager turns Pay by QR on in Taliup HQ. You need:

- The **Pay by QR** feature on the merchant's plan. Ask your ISO or partner if you don't see it.
- At least one location that takes online card payments, with an active [online payment gateway](/taliup-hq/onboarding/locations#online-payment-gateway).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open payment settings">
    In Taliup HQ, go to **Settings** → **Payments**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    In the **Pay by QR** card, turn on **Pay by QR**. You need the **Manage Settings** permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If no location takes online card payments, the switch stays off and you see **None of your locations take online card payments yet.** If only some do, the card lists the locations where the QR code is not printed.

QR codes print only on terminals at locations that take online card payments. Pay by QR is not available on devices enrolled with [WEB-SRM](/taliup-pos/web-srm).

Turning Pay by QR off also stops payments from bills already printed.

## Print a bill with a QR code

You can print the QR code two ways.

### The Pay by QR tender

Use it to hand the customer a bill and move on to the next sale.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the order">
    Add the items as usual.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Pay by QR">
    Tap the **Pay by QR** tender key. If it doesn't fit with the other keys, open **More payment types**. Taliup POS saves the order to Taliup HQ as an open order and prints the bill with its QR code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand over the bill">
    Taliup POS shows **Bill printed. The customer can scan the QR code to pay.** and clears the register for the next sale. The order stays open until the customer pays.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Pay by QR** is not shown on one guest's bill or on a sale that loads a gift card.

If Taliup POS cannot get a payment link from Taliup HQ, it shows **Couldn't get a payment link from HQ, so the bill wasn't printed.** Check the connection and try again.

### Print Entire Bill

On an open order, **Options** → **Print Entire Bill** also prints the QR code. The order is saved to Taliup HQ and stays open on the register so you can keep working on it. See [Options side menu](/taliup-pos/register/options).

The QR code prints under **Scan to pay** with **Pay this bill from your phone**. It prints on built-in and external printers. Individual guest bills do not carry a QR code, and neither does a bill printed while the register is offline.

## What the customer sees

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan the code">
    The customer scans the QR code. **Your bill** opens with the items, totals, anything already paid, and the **Amount due**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a tip">
    Under **Add a tip**, they pick **No tip**, 10%, 15%, 20%, or **Custom**. The tip can't be more than the bill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    They tap **Pay** with the amount and finish on a secure checkout page. Their card details never touch the bill page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    The page shows **Paid in full**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

- The customer pays only what is still owed. Payments already taken on the register are shown and left out.
- If the bill changes while the page is open, they see **Your bill was updated. Check the new total before you pay.**
- A declined card shows a message to try again or use another card.
- The link works for 7 days. After that, the customer sees **This link has expired** and asks staff for a new bill.

## When the order is paid online

The online payment goes on the same order in Taliup HQ.

When you next save or pay that order on the register, Taliup POS shows **This order was paid online. It's closed now.** and clears it. Before you take a payment on an order whose bill has a QR code, Taliup POS checks with Taliup HQ first. If Taliup HQ doesn't answer within a few seconds, the sale goes ahead.

Orders that were queued offline and synced later are also closed when they were paid online.

## If the cart changed after the customer paid

| Change | What happens |
|---|---|
| Items added | The online payment is added to the order. Taliup POS shows **The customer paid [amount] online before the order changed. [amount] is still owed.** Collect the rest |
| Items removed | The order closes. Taliup POS shows **This order was paid online and is closed. The customer paid [amount] more than the new total, so refund the difference.** |
