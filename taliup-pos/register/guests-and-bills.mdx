A table order keeps items per guest: **Guest 1**, **Guest 2** and so on, plus the whole table. You can move or split items between guests, print a bill for each guest, and take each guest's payment separately.

## Before you start

- Guests exist on table orders. Your business needs the **Table Management** feature; the number of guests comes from the party size. See [Tables](/taliup-pos/tables).
- Paid items cannot be moved or split. Partial items (for example a half quantity) cannot be split.

## Move items to another guest

1. In the cart, tap the guest header (a guest or **Whole Table**) and choose **Move Items**.
2. Select the items, or **Select all**.
3. Under **Move to**, pick the guest who should get them.
4. Tap **Move 1 item to …** (or **Move N items to …**).

## Split items between guests

Splitting shares an item's price evenly between several guests, for example a shared appetizer.

1. In the cart, tap the guest header and choose **Split Items**.
2. Select the items to share.
3. Under **Split between**, pick the guests (or **Select all**).
4. Tap **Split N items · N ways**.

## Print a bill per guest

- For one guest: tap the guest header and choose **Print Bill**.
- For several guests: tap **Order actions** (the menu icon) → **Print individual bills**, select the guests (or **Select all**), then tap **Print N bills**.

## Take a guest's payment

Tap the guest header and choose **Pay**. The payment screen opens for that guest's balance. See [Take payment](/taliup-pos/register/take-payment).

If a guest has no items, Taliup POS tells you so instead of opening the option.
