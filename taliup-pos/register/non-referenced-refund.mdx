**Create Refund** puts the Register in Refund mode, so you can refund a typed amount to a card without an original sale. When the original sale is in Transactions, refund it from there instead; see [Refund a transaction](/taliup-pos/transactions/refund).

## Before you start

- Your role needs the **Non-Ref Refunds** permission. Without it, **Create Refund** is greyed out and tapping it shows **You do not have permission to process refunds.**
- Refund mode refunds to a card only. The keys depend on your payment setup.
- On a WEB-SRM (Revenu Québec) register, the refund produces a credit note.

## Refund an amount

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Create Refund">
    In Register, go to the **Open Sale** tab, tap **Order actions** (the menu icon), then **Create Refund** in the **Bill** section. On the [Open Sale Only](/taliup-pos/register/open-sale-only) screen, tap **Options** → **Create Refund**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Refund mode">
    On **Switch to Refund Mode**, tap **Enter Refund Mode**. The cart and any entered amount are cleared. A **Refund** banner shows you are in Refund mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Type the amount before tax on the keypad. Taxes selected with the **Tax** switch are added on top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a card key">
    Tap **Credit** or **Debit**, or **Debit/Credit** when your terminal detects the card type. **EBT** appears when your business accepts it, and **Manual entry** on Elavon card readers. There is no tip, surcharge or dual pricing on a refund.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Present the card">
    Follow the card reader. The refund appears in [Transactions](/taliup-pos/transactions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To return to normal sales, tap **Exit** in the **Refund** banner, then **Enter Payment Mode**.
