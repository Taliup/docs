**Inventory** tracks stock for sellable products and, when enabled, raw materials and units. Access requires the inventory feature.

<Frame caption="Inventory products list with Name, SKU, QTY.">
  ![Inventory products list with Name, SKU, QTY](/images/taliup-pos/catalog/inventory.png)
</Frame>

## How to get here

Home → **Menu** / **Catalog** → **Inventory**.

## Navigation

| Section | Availability |
|---|---|
| **Products** | Always (within Inventory) |
| **Raw Materials** | Typically restaurants; may require an inventory add-on |
| **Units** | When units feature is enabled |

## Products

List columns: **Name**, **SKU**, **QTY.** Search by name, SKU, or price.

Open a product to edit inventory:

- Quantity on hand
- **RP.** (reorder point) when enabled
- Optional **Inventory Measurement** (unit + quantity deducted per sale) when units are enabled
- **Weighable** plus a **kg**, **g**, **lb**, or **oz** unit when the product is sold from a scale sticker — see [Scale barcodes](/taliup-pos/register/scale-barcodes)

Options may include syncing inventory with products. Save or cancel your changes.

## Raw materials

Title **Raw Materials**. Tap **Add Raw Material** or edit a row.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Material name |
| **Unit** | Unit of measure |
| **Qty on Hand** | Adjustable quantity |
| **Inventory Threshold** | Low-stock threshold |

Search by name. Delete is available when editing.

## Units

Title **Units**. Tap **Add Custom Unit** or edit a row.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Unit name |
| **Abbreviation** | For example kg |
| **Precision** | Decimal precision |

Search by name or abbreviation.
