<Update label="September 2026" tags={["Taliup POS", "Taliup HQ"]}>

**New**

- **Choose customer receipt** — after a sale, staff or the customer picks **No Receipt**, **Paper**, or **Email**. Covers split payments and full-order receipts. [Customer receipt](/taliup-pos/register/customer-receipt)
- **Scale barcodes and weighable items** — scan a price or weight label from a counter scale into the Register cart, or type a weight. Set the SKU, price per unit, and **Weighable** flag in Taliup HQ. [Scale barcodes](/taliup-pos/register/scale-barcodes) · [Scale items](/taliup-hq/features/scale-items)
- **Recurring Payments** — create an agreement, send a hosted checkout link, and collect scheduled payments from the merchant portal. [Recurring Payments](/taliup-hq/features/recurring-payments)
- **End of Day in Taliup HQ** — generate the merchant daily document in the browser for one or more terminals and employees. [End of Day](/taliup-hq/features/end-of-day)
- **View all terminal transactions** — a new Taliup HQ permission that lets a user see every terminal's transactions in the Transactions list.

**Improved**

- **End of Day on the terminal** now reports on multiple devices and users at once. [End of Day](/taliup-pos/transactions/end-of-day)
- **Default screen after login** — each terminal can open Home or Register after a passcode. [General settings](/taliup-pos/settings/general)
- **Auto-Print Customer Receipt** prints the customer copy and skips the receipt prompt. [General settings](/taliup-pos/settings/general)
- The **Orders** list now loads the last 60 days.
- Demo mode caps transactions at $1.

**Fixed**

- **Guest and rest-of-table receipts** — guest Pay Full and rest-of-table pays show Email and itemize only what was paid; guest splits print a payment slip, then **Guest Fully Paid**. [Customer receipt](/taliup-pos/register/customer-receipt)
