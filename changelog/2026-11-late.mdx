<Update label="Late November 2026" tags={["Taliup POS", "Taliup HQ"]}>

**New**

- **Online booking** — a redesigned booking page where clients sign in with Google or a code sent to their email, pay a deposit or pick one of their saved cards, and book visits their membership covers. **See the whole month** opens the full calendar when picking a date, and a time someone else just booked can no longer be paid for twice. Booking emails have a new design. [Online booking](/taliup-hq/features/online-booking)
- **Appointment payments** — cancelling or marking a no-show settles the deposit and fees in one step. Send a deposit payment link from HQ or the Taliup POS booking form, even after the appointment is booked, and check out an appointment on Taliup POS with the online deposit already applied. When cancelling in Taliup HQ, add a reason that only your team sees. [Calendar](/taliup-hq/features/calendar) · [Appointments](/taliup-pos/calendar/appointments)
- **Memberships** — bill clients for a plan every period through Recurring Payments, with included services and member discounts at the Register, on the Virtual Terminal and on invoices. Sign clients up in Taliup HQ or on Taliup POS. [Memberships](/taliup-hq/features/memberships) · [Memberships on Taliup POS](/taliup-pos/register/packages-and-memberships)
- **Pay by QR** — print a QR code on the bill so customers pay what they owe, plus a tip, from their phone. The order closes once paid. [Pay by QR](/taliup-pos/register/pay-by-qr)
- **Pay runs and commissions** — add up hours, commissions and tips for each pay period, with tiered and per-employee commission rates and Helped by credit, then export for payroll. [Pay runs and commissions](/taliup-hq/features/payroll)

**Improved**

- **Redesigned Taliup HQ** — a new side menu, and every page rebuilt with a cleaner look, team pages included. The calendar has new views, drawers to book and review appointments, drag to reschedule, and rooms, equipment (with how many each service needs) and prep time per service. An appointment's History lists the client's earlier visits, and Calendar settings are grouped into four tabs with a summary at the top. [Calendar](/taliup-hq/features/calendar) · [Calendar settings](/taliup-hq/features/calendar-settings) · [Employees](/taliup-hq/features/employees) · [Timesheets](/taliup-hq/features/timesheets) · [Staff schedule](/taliup-hq/features/staff-schedule)
- **Emailed receipts** — a new design in the business's colours. A split sale lists the order once, then each payment.

**Fixed**

- **Recurring payment and membership emails** — sign-up, update and declined emails now use the business's language and the client's name, and name the membership plan when there is one. [Memberships](/taliup-hq/features/memberships)
- **Receipt payment times** — cash payments on emailed receipts showed the wrong time. Every payment now shows the business's local time.
