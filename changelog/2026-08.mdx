<Update label="August 2026" tags={["Taliup POS", "Taliup HQ"]}>

**New**

- **WEB-SRM (Revenu Québec)** on Taliup POS — enable it in Taliup HQ, confirm the app version, then log out and enrol each device. [WEB-SRM](/taliup-pos/web-srm) · [Existing merchants](/taliup-pos/web-srm/switch-live-merchant)
- **Migrate from Taliup MEV** — move a merchant off the standalone Taliup MEV app onto Taliup POS. [Migrate from MEV](/taliup-hq/onboarding/web-srm#migrate-from-mev)
- **Charity receipt actions** on hosted payments. [Hosted payments](/taliup-hq/features/hosted-payments)
- **Restore a deleted client** in Taliup HQ. [Clients](/taliup-hq/features/clients)

**Improved**

- Locations support for Poynt terminals. [Locations](/taliup-hq/onboarding/locations)
- Taliup HQ and Taliup POS documentation is now available in French (fr-CA).
