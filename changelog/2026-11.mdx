<Update label="November 2026" tags={["Taliup POS", "Taliup HQ"]}>

**New**

- **Loyalty** — customers earn points on paid sales and redeem them as a discount at the Register. Enrol with a printed or digital card; members get a card page and Apple Wallet or Google Wallet passes. Set the rules, welcome bonus, and cards in Taliup HQ. [Loyalty](/taliup-pos/register/loyalty) · [Loyalty in Taliup HQ](/taliup-hq/features/loyalty)
- **Gift Cards** — sell, reload, and check physical or digital gift cards at the Register, and accept them as payment. Refunds go back onto the card. CAD merchants can order printed cards in Taliup HQ. [Gift cards](/taliup-pos/register/gift-cards) · [Gift Cards in Taliup HQ](/taliup-hq/features/gift-cards)
- **Floor plans** — group tables into floor plans by room, switch between floor plan and list views, and arrange tables with pinch, rotate, and an alignment grid. Also in Taliup HQ under **Table Management**. [Tables](/taliup-pos/tables#floor-plans)
- **Helped by** — credit a sale to the employee who served the customer. Tips and Sales by employee follow that person. [Helped by](/taliup-pos/register/helped-by)
- **Half-and-half options** — put a topping on the left half, right half, or whole item. [Add items](/taliup-pos/register/add-items#half-and-half-options-sides)
- **Receipt barcode** — print a Code 128 barcode on receipts, then scan it or search the token to open the sale. Off by default. [Receipt barcode](/taliup-pos/register/receipt-barcode)
- **Getting started** — a first-run guide for the card reader, printer, a first sale, and Settings. Reopen it from Settings → Tools. [Getting started](/taliup-pos/settings/getting-started)
- **Manual card entry** — key in card details from the payment screen. [Take payment](/taliup-pos/register/take-payment#other-methods)
- **Share Analytics** — send the Analytics summary as a PDF by QR code or email. [Analytics](/taliup-pos/analytics#share-the-summary)
- **What's new in the app** — after an update, Taliup POS shows what changed. Tap the app version in Settings to read it again. [Settings](/taliup-pos/settings)

**Improved**

- **Redesigned Taliup POS** — a new look across every screen. Printer status now opens in a bottom sheet.
- **Payment screen** — tender keys show the amount they will charge, cash always offers three different suggestions, and the tip prompt shows a fourth preset with **Custom** below. [Take payment](/taliup-pos/register/take-payment)
- **Taliup HQ sync** — see sales waiting on this register and send them with **Sync now** from Settings → **Tools**. Clear Application Data warns before deleting anything HQ has not received. [General settings](/taliup-pos/settings/general#taliup-hq-sync)
- **Calendar** — warns when an appointment overlaps another, offers service durations up to 8 hours, and saves the location you pick. [Appointments](/taliup-pos/calendar/appointments)
- **Past favorites** — after you attach a client, add items they ordered in the last 3 months. [Attach from Register](/taliup-pos/clients/attach-from-register)
