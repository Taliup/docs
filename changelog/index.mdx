What shipped, newest first. Each entry covers one release across every terminal.
Version numbers differ by channel because each one is distributed through its own
store — a feature listed here reaches every channel, on the dates shown under
**Availability**.

To find your version on a terminal, go to Home → **Settings** → **Device**.
