What shipped, newest first. Each entry covers one release across every terminal.
Version numbers differ by channel because each one is distributed through its own
store — a feature listed here reaches every channel, on the dates shown under
**Availability**.

## Find your version

On a terminal, open **Settings**. The installed version is shown at the bottom, for example **Version 2.0.13 (31)**. On an Open Sale Only business, open Settings from **Options** → **Open Settings**.

To see what changed in your version, tap the version at the bottom of Settings.

## Which availability row applies

Each release lists one row per channel. PAX terminals with Nuvei or Elavon run one of two app lines: check whether your version starts with **2.** or **1.**, then use the matching **PAX (Nuvei, Elavon) 2.x** or **PAX (Nuvei, Elavon) 1.x** row. A channel that is not listed yet may still be rolling out; its row is added once its version and date are confirmed.
